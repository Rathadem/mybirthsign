Khmer Culture & Calendar

Khmer Leap Months and Leap Days

✦ why some Khmer years have 384 days, and how that moves the festivals ✦

Twelve moons make only about 354 days, eleven short of a solar year. The Khmer calendar fixes this by sometimes adding one day and sometimes a whole second month of Asadh. 2026 is one of those leap-month years, and it is the reason Pchum Ben came so late this year.

Published October 18, 2026 · MyBirthSign

Three kinds of Khmer year

  • Normal year · 354 days

    Twelve months that alternate between 29 and 30 days.

  • Leap-day year · 355 days

    Called Chantreathimeas. The month of Jesth gets 30 days instead of 29, keeping the months in step with the real moon.

  • Leap-month year · 384 days

    Called Athikameas. Asadh is repeated: Pathamasadh (1st Asadh) and Tutiyasadh (2nd Asadh), 30 days each, which keeps the festivals in their season.

Why the calendar needs leap months

A month runs from one new moon to the next, about 29½ days. Twelve of them come to about 354 days, but the sun takes about 365¼ days to bring the seasons round. Without a correction, Khmer months would drift eleven days earlier every year and, after about 16 years, Pchum Ben would fall in the hot season.

Adding a 30-day month every two or three years pulls the calendar back. Over 19 solar years the gap adds up to almost exactly seven months, because 19 solar years and 235 lunar months (19 × 12 + 7) are both about 6,940 days. In every 19-year stretch from 1990 to 2045, the calendar rules give exactly seven leap-month years.

The leap day does a smaller job. A lunar month is a little longer than 29½ days, so a 354-day year runs about a third of a day short of twelve real moons. Every few years Jesth gets a 30th day to make that up.

2026: a year with two Asadh

In 2026 the first Asadh ran from 15 June to 14 July and the second Asadh from 15 July to 13 August. The rains retreat follows the second Asadh in a leap-month year, so Chol Vassa came on 30 July 2026, nineteen days later than in 2025.

Everything after it moved too: Pchum Ben day was 22 September in 2025, 11 October in 2026, and the calendar rules give 30 September for 2027. Read more in our guide to Chol Vassa.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Athikameas?

Athikameas is a Khmer leap-month year. The month of Asadh is repeated, as Pathamasadh (1st Asadh) and Tutiyasadh (2nd Asadh), so the year has 13 months and 384 days.

What is Chantreathimeas?

Chantreathimeas is a Khmer leap-day year: the month of Jesth has 30 days instead of 29, and the year has 355 days.

Is 2026 a leap year in the Khmer calendar?

Yes. 2026 has a leap month: the second Asadh ran from 15 July to 13 August 2026.

Is 2027 a Khmer leap year?

No. The calendar rules give 2027 as a normal year, with one Asadh from 4 July to 2 August. The next leap month comes in 2029.

Why was Pchum Ben so late in 2026?

Because of the second Asadh. Every month after it was pushed back by 30 days compared with a normal year, so Pchum Ben came on 11 October instead of in September.

Why is the extra month always Asadh?

That is the rule of the traditional Khmer calendar: in a leap-month year Asadh is the month that repeats, and the rains retreat, Chol Vassa, then begins after the second Asadh.

高棉文化与历法

高棉历的闰月与闰日

✦ 为什么有些高棉年有384天，以及它如何让节日移动 ✦

十二次月圆只有约354天，比一个太阳年少11天。高棉历的解决办法是在某些年份增加一天，或者增加整整一个第二 Asadh 月。2026年正是这样的闰月年，这也是今年亡人节来得这么晚的原因。

三种高棉年

  • 平年 · 354天

    十二个月，在29天和30天之间交替。

  • 闰日年 · 355天

    称为 Chantreathimeas。Jesth 月有30天而不是29天，使月份与真实月相保持一致。

  • 闰月年 · 384天

    称为 Athikameas。Asadh 月重复一次：Pathamasadh（第一个 Asadh）和 Tutiyasadh（第二个 Asadh），各30天，使节日保持在应有的季节。

为什么历法需要闰月

一个月从一次新月到下一次新月，约29天半。十二个月合计约354天，但太阳要约365又1/4天才让季节轮回一次。如果不加修正，高棉月份每年会提前11天，大约16年后亡人节就会落在热季。

每两三年加一个30天的月，就能把历法拉回来。19个太阳年中，差距累积起来几乎正好是七个月，因为19个太阳年和235个朔望月（19 × 12 + 7）都约为6,940天。按历法规则，从1990年到2045年的任何连续19年里，都正好有七个闰月年。

闰日的作用较小。一个朔望月比29天半略长，所以354天的一年比十二个真实月相少约三分之一天。每隔几年，Jesth 月就会多出第30天来补足。

2026年：有两个 Asadh 月的一年

2026年，第一个 Asadh 月从6月15日到7月14日，第二个 Asadh 月从7月15日到8月13日。在闰月年，守夏安居跟随第二个 Asadh 月，所以2026年的入夏节在7月30日，比2025年晚了19天。

之后的一切也随之推后：亡人节当天在2025年是9月22日，2026年是10月11日，按历法规则2027年是9月30日。详见我们的入夏节指南。

常见问题

什么是 Athikameas？

Athikameas 是高棉的闰月年。Asadh 月重复一次，分为 Pathamasadh（第一个 Asadh）和 Tutiyasadh（第二个 Asadh），所以这一年有13个月、384天。

什么是 Chantreathimeas？

Chantreathimeas 是高棉的闰日年：Jesth 月有30天而不是29天，全年355天。

2026年是高棉历的闰年吗？

是的。2026年有闰月：第二个 Asadh 月从2026年7月15日到8月13日。

2027年是高棉闰年吗？

不是。按历法规则，2027年是平年，只有一个 Asadh 月，从7月4日到8月2日。下一个闰月在2029年。

为什么2026年的亡人节这么晚？

因为有第二个 Asadh 月。与平年相比，它之后的每个月都推后了30天，所以亡人节在10月11日，而不是九月。

为什么闰月总是 Asadh？

这是传统高棉历法的规则：在闰月年，重复的月份就是 Asadh，而守夏安居（入夏节）在第二个 Asadh 月之后开始。
