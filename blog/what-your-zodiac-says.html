Chinese Zodiac Guide

What Your Chinese Zodiac Sign Says About Your Personality

✦ Strengths, challenges and best matches for all 12 signs ✦

The animal of your birth year is said to shape how you approach relationships, work and decisions. Find your sign below.

Updated October 11, 2026 · MyBirthSign

The Chinese zodiac is a 12-year cycle, with each year represented by an animal: Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Goat, Monkey, Rooster, Dog and Pig. According to tradition, the animal that rules the year of your birth leaves a lasting mark on your personality.

Unlike Western astrology, which follows the sun’s position at your birth, the Chinese zodiac is tied to your birth year — counted from Lunar New Year, not 1 January. That makes it simple to look up, while the traits of each animal have been refined over centuries of folklore and observation.

Four Personality Groups

  • The leaders (Tiger · Dragon · Horse)

    Tigers are brave and competitive, often taking charge of a room without trying. Dragons are thought of as the most charismatic of the twelve, linked to ambition and big ideas. Horses bring restless, adventurous energy and often thrive on travel, movement and change.

  • The thinkers (Snake · Rabbit · Ox)

    Snakes are wise and intuitive, preferring to observe before acting. Rabbits are gentle diplomats, often the peacemakers of their circle. Oxen are famous for patience and reliability — slow to anger, and even slower to give up once committed.

  • The connectors (Rat · Monkey · Goat)

    Rats are resourceful and quick to spot opportunities, often doing well in business. Monkeys are clever and witty, bringing humor and creative problem-solving. Goats (sometimes called Sheep) are compassionate and artistic, deeply aware of the people around them.

  • The steady ones (Rooster · Dog · Pig)

    Roosters are detail-oriented and take pride in doing things properly. Dogs are fiercely loyal and protective, first to stand up for a friend. Pigs are warm, generous and easygoing, valuing comfort and honest relationships over status.

Your Sign Is a Starting Point, Not a Script

Zodiac traits describe tendencies, not guarantees. Two people born in the same animal year can turn out very differently depending on upbringing, experience and choices. Many people find it most useful as a lens for reflection — a way to notice patterns in their own behavior — rather than a fixed label.

中国生肖以十二年为一个周期，每一年由一个动物代表：鼠、牛、虎、兔、龙、蛇、马、羊、猴、鸡、狗、猪。按照传统，你出生那年的生肖会在你的性格上留下持久的印记。

西方占星依据你出生时太阳的位置，而中国生肖与出生年份相关——从农历新年起算，而不是1月1日。所以生肖很容易查到，而每个生肖的性格特点则是经过几百年的民间传说和观察逐渐形成的。

广告位

四大性格类型

  • 领导者 （虎 · 龙 · 马）

    虎勇敢好胜，常常不费力气就成为全场的主导。龙被认为是十二生肖中最有魅力的，与抱负和远大想法联系在一起。马带来不安分、爱冒险的能量，常在旅行、奔走和变化中如鱼得水。

  • 思考者 （蛇 · 兔 · 牛）

    蛇睿智而有直觉，喜欢先观察再行动。兔是温和的外交家，常是朋友圈里的和事佬。牛以耐心和可靠著称——不轻易发怒，一旦投入更不轻易放弃。

  • 连接者 （鼠 · 猴 · 羊）

    鼠足智多谋，善于发现机会，常在生意上表现出色。猴聪明风趣，带来幽默和有创意的解决办法。羊富有同情心和艺术气质，对身边的人非常敏感。

  • 稳健者 （鸡 · 狗 · 猪）

    鸡注重细节，以把事情做好为荣。狗极其忠诚、有保护欲，总是第一个站出来维护朋友。猪温暖、慷慨、随和，重视舒适和真诚的关系胜过地位。

你的生肖是起点，而不是剧本

生肖性格描述的是倾向，而不是保证。同一生肖年出生的两个人，可能因为成长环境、经历和选择而截然不同。很多人觉得它最有用的地方，是作为一种自我反思的视角——帮助察觉自己行为中的规律——而不是一个固定的标签。
