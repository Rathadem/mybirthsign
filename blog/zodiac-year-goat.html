🐐 Goat Chinese Zodiac: Personality, Compatibility & Today's Fortune

Ad space

If you were born in one of these years, you belong to the Goat: 1967, 1979, 1991, 2003, 2015, 2027, 2039 (the cycle repeats every 12 years, so the pattern continues both before and after).

The Goat (sometimes translated as Sheep) is the zodiac's gentle artist, drawn to beauty, harmony, and emotional depth. Goats tend to notice feelings that others miss, making them warm friends and genuinely talented in creative fields. They do their best work in a secure, supportive environment, and tend to struggle more in cutthroat or highly competitive settings. Left without that support, a Goat can become indecisive or lean too heavily on others to make the hard calls for them.

Personality

Gentle, creative, and compassionate. Goats have a strong artistic sensibility and care deeply for others.

Goats notice beauty and emotional undercurrents that most people walk right past, which makes them unusually good company and genuinely gifted at creative or caretaking work. They flourish fastest around people who are patient with their sensitivity rather than impatient for them to toughen up.

In relationships

Goats are warm, attentive partners who put real thought into making the people they love feel cared for. They do best with a partner who can take the lead on hard practical decisions sometimes, since a Goat's instinct under pressure is to look to someone else rather than decide alone.

At work

Goats bring real craft and emotional intelligence to creative or people-focused work, and tend to do their best in supportive, low-conflict teams. Cutthroat, highly competitive workplaces usually bring out their worst, not their best.

What to watch for

Can be indecisive or overly dependent on others. Left to make a hard call alone, a Goat can freeze up or quietly hand the decision to someone else — a little practice making small decisions solo tends to help.

Goats bond most easily with warm, patient signs who create the secure environment they need to flourish, and struggle more with blunt or highly competitive signs.
