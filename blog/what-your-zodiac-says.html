Chinese Zodiac Guide

What Your Chinese Zodiac Sign Says About Your Personality

✦ Strengths, challenges and best matches for all 12 signs ✦

The animal of your birth year is said to shape how you approach relationships, work and decisions. Find your sign below.

Updated October 11, 2026 · MyBirthSign

The Chinese zodiac is a 12-year cycle, with each year represented by an animal: Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Goat, Monkey, Rooster, Dog and Pig. According to tradition, the animal that rules the year of your birth leaves a lasting mark on your personality.

Unlike Western astrology, which follows the sun’s position at your birth, the Chinese zodiac is tied to your birth year — counted from Lunar New Year, not 1 January. That makes it simple to look up, while the traits of each animal have been refined over centuries of folklore and observation.

The Four Traditional Triangles

Tradition groups the twelve animals into four triangles of three (sān hé). The animals in a triangle sit four places apart on the zodiac wheel and are said to share a similar pace, which is why they count as each other’s best matches. Each triangle is also linked with one element.

  • The quick-starters (Rat · Dragon · Monkey — Water triangle)

    Rats spot openings early, Dragons bring ambition and big ideas, and Monkeys find inventive ways through problems. Tradition sees them as fast-moving signs that energise each other.

  • The planners (Ox · Snake · Rooster — Metal triangle)

    Oxen are patient and dependable, Snakes think before they act, and Roosters take pride in doing things properly. They share a liking for preparation and high standards.

  • The idealists (Tiger · Horse · Dog — Fire triangle)

    Tigers are brave and competitive, Horses restless and adventurous, and Dogs fiercely loyal. What links them is strong conviction and a readiness to act for what they believe in.

  • The peacemakers (Rabbit · Goat · Pig — Wood triangle)

    Rabbits are gentle diplomats, Goats compassionate and artistic, and Pigs warm and generous. They value harmony, comfort and kindness over competition.

The nicknames above are our own summary; the triangles themselves are the traditional part. Our compatibility guide explains how they, the six harmony pairs and the clashes are used.

Your Sign Is a Starting Point, Not a Script

Zodiac traits describe tendencies, not guarantees. Two people born in the same animal year can turn out very differently depending on upbringing, experience and choices. Many people find it most useful as a lens for reflection — a way to notice patterns in their own behavior — rather than a fixed label.

中国生肖以十二年为一个周期，每一年由一个动物代表：鼠、牛、虎、兔、龙、蛇、马、羊、猴、鸡、狗、猪。按照传统，你出生那年的生肖会在你的性格上留下持久的印记。

四组传统三合

传统上，十二生肖分为四组三合。同一组的三个生肖在生肖轮上相隔四位，被认为步调相近，所以被视为彼此最相配的组合。每组三合也对应一种五行。

  • 行动派 (鼠 · 龙 · 猴——水局 申子辰)

    鼠善于抢先发现机会，龙带来雄心和大格局，猴总能想出巧妙的办法解决问题。传统上认为他们节奏快，能互相激发活力。

  • 计划派 (牛 · 蛇 · 鸡——金局 巳酉丑)

    牛耐心可靠，蛇三思而后行，鸡做事讲究、追求做好。他们都重视准备和高标准。

  • 理想派 (虎 · 马 · 狗——火局 寅午戌)

    虎勇敢好胜，马好动爱冒险，狗极其忠诚。把他们联系在一起的是坚定的信念，以及为信念付诸行动的决心。

  • 和平派 (兔 · 羊 · 猪——木局 亥卯未)

    兔是温和的协调者，羊富有同情心和艺术气质，猪热情大方。他们看重和谐、舒适与善意，而不是竞争。

上面的称呼是我们的概括，三合本身才是传统的部分。生肖配对指南介绍了三合、六合与六冲的用法。

你的生肖是起点，而不是剧本

生肖性格描述的是倾向，而不是保证。同一生肖年出生的两个人，可能因为成长环境、经历和选择而截然不同。很多人觉得它最有用的地方，是作为一种自我反思的视角——帮助察觉自己行为中的规律——而不是一个固定的标签。
