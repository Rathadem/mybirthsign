2025 Chinese Zodiac: Year of the Wood Snake

Born in 2025? Your Chinese zodiac animal is most likely the Snake, but check the date: the 2025 Wood Snake year began on 29 January 2025. Here is everything about the year, from its element to its lucky colors and best matches.

2025: a Yin Wood Snake year

The Chinese zodiac year 2025 was the Year of the Snake. It began with Lunar New Year on 29 January 2025 and ended on 16 February 2026, the eve of the next Lunar New Year.

Each year also has an element and a Yin or Yang quality. 2025 ends in 5, which makes it a Wood year, and as an odd year it is Yin. In the traditional stem-and-branch count, 2025 is a Yi-si year. The same Wood Snake combination comes round only once every 60 years: 1965 before it and 2085 after it.

Born in January or February 2025?

Lunar New Year 2025 fell on 29 January 2025. Anyone born from 1 January 2025 up to the day before belongs to the previous year's animal, the Dragon; from 29 January 2025 on, the Snake.

The zodiac year starts at Lunar New Year, not on 1 January, which is why early-year birthdays need checking. Our guide Born in January? explains the rule, and the zodiac sign checker gives the exact answer for any birth date.

The Snake's personality

Our Snake guide describes the sign like this: “Wise, intuitive, and elegant. Snakes are deep thinkers who plan carefully before acting.” The usual watch-out: “Can be secretive or prone to overthinking.”

The Wood element adds its own flavour. Wood years bring growth, creativity, and a drive to build new things. People of this element tend to be cooperative, generous, and idealistic. Read more in our Wood element guide, or the full Year of the Snake page.

Careers often linked with the Snake: finance, research, psychology, strategy.

Lucky colors, numbers and days for the Snake

  • Lucky colors: black, red and yellow
  • Lucky numbers: 2, 8 and 9
  • Lucky days: Wednesday and Saturday

These are the values used across this site. For a lucky color, number and time that change every day, see the daily fortune.

Best matches for someone born in 2025

In traditional compatibility, the Snake's best matches are the Ox, Rooster and Monkey, and its clash sign is the Pig, which sits directly opposite it on the zodiac circle. Our compatibility calculator compares two full birth dates, elements included.

The Snake in 2026 and 2027

2026 (Fire Horse, until 5 February 2027): for the Snake, it has no special traditional relationship with the Horse, so 2026 is a neutral year.

2027 (Fire Goat, from 6 February 2027): for the Snake, it has no special traditional relationship with the Goat, so 2027 is a neutral year. See Chinese zodiac 2027 predictions for every sign.

Other Year of the Snake years

Years of the same animal come every twelve years, each with a different element: 2001 (Metal Snake); 2013 (Water Snake); 2037 (Fire Snake). The full list is in our Chinese zodiac years chart.
