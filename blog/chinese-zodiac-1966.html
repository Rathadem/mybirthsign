1966 Chinese Zodiac: Year of the Fire Horse

Born in 1966? Your Chinese zodiac animal is most likely the Horse, but check the date: the 1966 Fire Horse year began on 21 January 1966. Here is everything about the year, from its element to its lucky colors and best matches.

1966: a Yang Fire Horse year

The Chinese zodiac year 1966 was the Year of the Horse. It began with Lunar New Year on 21 January 1966 and ended on 8 February 1967, the eve of the next Lunar New Year.

Each year also has an element and a Yin or Yang quality. 1966 ends in 6, which makes it a Fire year, and as an even year it is Yang. In the traditional stem-and-branch count, 1966 is a Bing-wu year. The same Fire Horse combination comes round only once every 60 years: 1906 before it and 2026 after it.

Born in January or February 1966?

Lunar New Year 1966 fell on 21 January 1966. Anyone born from 1 January 1966 up to the day before belongs to the previous year's animal, the Snake; from 21 January 1966 on, the Horse.

The zodiac year starts at Lunar New Year, not on 1 January, which is why early-year birthdays need checking. Our guide Born in January? explains the rule, and the zodiac sign checker gives the exact answer for any birth date.

The Horse's personality

Our Horse guide describes the sign like this: “Energetic, independent, and adventurous. Horses love freedom and new experiences.” The usual watch-out: “Can be impatient or struggle to commit long-term.”

The Fire element adds its own flavour. Fire years bring passion, energy, and boldness. People of this element tend to be dynamic, adventurous, and quick to lead. Read more in our Fire element guide, or the full Year of the Horse page.

Careers often linked with the Horse: travel, sales, sports, media.

Lucky colors, numbers and days for the Horse

  • Lucky colors: yellow, green and purple
  • Lucky numbers: 2, 3 and 7
  • Lucky days: Monday and Wednesday

Best matches for someone born in 1966

In traditional compatibility, the Horse's best matches are the Tiger, Goat and Dog, and its clash sign is the Rat, which sits directly opposite it on the zodiac circle. Our compatibility calculator compares two full birth dates, elements included.

The Horse in 2026 and 2027

2026 (Fire Horse, until 5 February 2027): for the Horse, 2026 is a Horse year, so Horse people are in their own year (ben ming nian) until 5 February 2027.

2027 (Fire Goat, from 6 February 2027): for the Horse, it is listed as a supportive match of the Goat, so 2027 is traditionally a friendly year. See Chinese zodiac 2027 predictions for every sign.
