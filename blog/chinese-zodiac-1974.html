1974 Chinese Zodiac: Year of the Wood Tiger

Born in 1974? Your Chinese zodiac animal is most likely the Tiger, but check the date: the 1974 Wood Tiger year began on 23 January 1974. Here is everything about the year, from its element to its lucky colors and best matches.

1974: a Yang Wood Tiger year

The Chinese zodiac year 1974 was the Year of the Tiger. It began with Lunar New Year on 23 January 1974 and ended on 10 February 1975, the eve of the next Lunar New Year.

Each year also has an element and a Yin or Yang quality. 1974 ends in 4, which makes it a Wood year, and as an even year it is Yang. In the traditional stem-and-branch count, 1974 is a Jia-yin year. The same Wood Tiger combination comes round only once every 60 years: 1914 before it and 2034 after it.

Born in January or February 1974?

Lunar New Year 1974 fell on 23 January 1974. Anyone born from 1 January 1974 up to the day before belongs to the previous year's animal, the Ox; from 23 January 1974 on, the Tiger.

The zodiac year starts at Lunar New Year, not on 1 January, which is why early-year birthdays need checking. Our guide Born in January? explains the rule, and the zodiac sign checker gives the exact answer for any birth date.

The Tiger's personality

Our Tiger guide describes the sign like this: “Brave, confident, and competitive. Tigers are natural leaders who act decisively.” The usual watch-out: “Can be impulsive or impatient with slower-moving people.”

The Wood element adds its own flavour. Wood years bring growth, creativity, and a drive to build new things. People of this element tend to be cooperative, generous, and idealistic. Read more in our Wood element guide, or the full Year of the Tiger page.

Careers often linked with the Tiger: entrepreneurship, sports, law, management.

Lucky colors, numbers and days for the Tiger

  • Lucky colors: blue, gray and orange
  • Lucky numbers: 1, 3 and 4
  • Lucky days: Tuesday and Wednesday

Best matches for someone born in 1974

In traditional compatibility, the Tiger's best matches are the Horse, Dog and Pig, and its clash sign is the Monkey, which sits directly opposite it on the zodiac circle. Our compatibility calculator compares two full birth dates, elements included.

The Tiger in 2026 and 2027

2026 (Fire Horse, until 5 February 2027): for the Tiger, it shares the Horse's triangle, so 2026 is traditionally one of its friendlier years.

2027 (Fire Goat, from 6 February 2027): for the Tiger, it has no special traditional relationship with the Goat, so 2027 is a neutral year. See Chinese zodiac 2027 predictions for every sign.
