The Spirit of the Tiger

The Tiger traditionally represents courage, strength, confidence, and determination.

Understanding the Tiger

The Tiger is the zodiac's natural risk-taker — bold, magnetic, and unafraid to go first. In rooms full of hesitation, the Tiger is usually the one who speaks up or makes the move everyone else was quietly considering. That courage makes them compelling leaders and exciting to be around, but it can also tip into impatience with process, rules, or people who need more time to catch up. Tigers do best when they pair their instinct for action with just enough restraint to bring others along.

Personality

Brave, confident, and competitive. Tigers are natural leaders who act decisively.

Tigers have a low tolerance for sitting on the sidelines — they'd rather make a decision and adjust later than wait for perfect information that may never come. That instinct makes them magnetic in a crisis, pulling a room together around a plan when everyone else is still debating.

In relationships

Tigers love boldly and directly, and want a partner who can keep up with their energy and meet their honesty with honesty of their own. They're fiercely protective of people they care about, though their competitive streak can turn small disagreements into contests neither partner actually wanted to have.

Check Love Compatibility →

At work

Tigers thrive in roles that reward initiative and quick decisions — starting something new, leading a team through a crisis, closing a deal. They can get restless in slow-moving bureaucracies, and do best when given real ownership rather than a long approval chain.

What to watch for

Can be impulsive or impatient with slower-moving people. That same drive can come across as steamrolling quieter voices, so a Tiger is well served by pausing to ask what the room actually thinks before announcing the decision.

Compatibility

Tigers pair most naturally with other high-energy, independent signs who won't be intimidated by their intensity, and clash hardest with signs who move at a far more cautious pace.

Best matches: Horse, Dog, Pig

Can clash with: Monkey

Careers that often fit well

Tigers tend to do best in work that rewards bold decisions and visible leadership rather than slow consensus-building.

Entrepreneurship, sports, law, management

Frequently Asked Questions

What years are the Year of the Tiger?

Tiger years include 1962, 1974, 1986, 1998, 2010, 2022, 2034. The zodiac repeats every 12 years, and the zodiac year begins at Lunar New Year, not on January 1.

What personality traits are associated with the Tiger?

Traditionally, the Tiger is associated with courage, confidence, independence, and determination. Brave, confident, and competitive. Tigers are natural leaders who act decisively.

What are the lucky colors for the Tiger?

The Tiger's lucky colors in our guide are Blue, Gray, Orange.

What are the lucky numbers for the Tiger?

The Tiger's lucky numbers are 1, 3, 4.

Which zodiac animals are compatible with the Tiger?

Best matches are Horse and Dog, which share the Tiger's compatibility triangle. Pig is also listed as a supportive match in our guide, while Monkey can be the most challenging pairing.

What element is my Tiger year?

It depends on your birth year — Wood: 1974, 2034 · Fire: 1986 · Earth: 1998 · Metal: 2010 · Water: 1962, 2022. Use the Zodiac Checker to confirm yours.

Does the Chinese zodiac year start on January 1?

No. The zodiac year begins at Lunar New Year, which falls between late January and mid-February. If you were born in January or February, check the Lunar New Year date or use our Zodiac Checker.

Is Chinese zodiac personality scientifically proven?

No. Chinese zodiac interpretations are traditional and cultural, and they are not scientifically proven. Enjoy them for fun and self-reflection.

សំណួរដែលសួរញឹកញាប់

តើឆ្នាំខាលគឺឆ្នាំអ្វីខ្លះ?

ឆ្នាំខាលរួមមាន 1962, 1974, 1986, 1998, 2010, 2022, 2034។ រង្វង់រាសីដដែលៗរៀងរាល់ ១២ឆ្នាំម្តង ហើយឆ្នាំរាសីចាប់ផ្តើមនៅថ្ងៃចូលឆ្នាំចន្ទគតិ មិនមែនថ្ងៃទី១ មករាទេ។

តើបុគ្គលិកលក្ខណៈអ្វីខ្លះដែលផ្សារភ្ជាប់នឹងខាល?

តាមប្រពៃណី ខាលត្រូវបានផ្សារភ្ជាប់ជាមួយភាពក្លាហាន ទំនុកចិត្ត ឯករាជ្យភាព និងការតាំងចិត្ត។ ក្លាហាន ជឿជាក់លើខ្លួនឯង និងចូលចិត្តប្រកួតប្រជែង។ ខាលជាអ្នកដឹកនាំធម្មជាតិដែលសម្រេចចិត្តដោយក្លាហាន។

តើពណ៌សំណាងរបស់ខាលជាអ្វី?

ពណ៌សំណាងរបស់ខាលក្នុងមគ្គុទ្ទេសក៍របស់យើងគឺ ខៀវ, ប្រផេះ, ទឹកក្រូច។

តើលេខសំណាងរបស់ខាលជាអ្វី?

លេខសំណាងរបស់ខាលគឺ 1, 3, 4។

តើសត្វណាខ្លះត្រូវគ្នាជាមួយខាល?

ត្រូវគ្នាបំផុតជាមួយ មមី, ច (ក្រុមតែមួយ)។ កុរ ក៏ត្រូវបានរាប់ជាដៃគូជួយគាំទ្រក្នុងមគ្គុទ្ទេសក៍របស់យើង។ វក អាចមានភាពប្រឈមជាងគេ។

តើខាលរបស់ខ្ញុំជាធាតុអ្វី?

ធាតុអាស្រ័យលើឆ្នាំកំណើតរបស់អ្នក — ឈើ៖ 1974, 2034 · ភ្លើង៖ 1986 · ដី៖ 1998 · លោហធាតុ៖ 2010 · ទឹក៖ 1962, 2022។ ប្រើម៉ាស៊ីនពិនិត្យរាសីដើម្បីបញ្ជាក់ឆ្នាំរបស់អ្នក។

តើឆ្នាំរាសីចាប់ផ្តើមថ្ងៃទី១ មករាឬទេ?

តើបុគ្គលិកលក្ខណៈតាមរាសីត្រូវបានបញ្ជាក់ដោយវិទ្យាសាស្ត្រឬទេ?
