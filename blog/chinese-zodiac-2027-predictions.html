Chinese Zodiac 2027 Predictions: The Fire Goat Year for Each Sign

The Fire Horse hands over to the Fire Goat on 6 February 2027. Here is what kind of year that is in the traditional calendar, and how each of the twelve signs is read in it, using the same rules as our compatibility tools.

What kind of year 2027 is

The Year of the Goat begins with Chinese New Year on Saturday 6 February 2027 and runs until 25 January 2028, the eve of the next Lunar New Year. The Hong Kong Observatory's conversion table for 2027 names it the Ding-wei year of the Goat.

In the stem-and-branch system, Ding is the Yin stem of the Fire element and Wei is the branch of the Goat, whose own element is Earth according to Wikipedia's article on the Goat. That is why 2027 is called a Fire Goat year. The same pairing returns every sixty years: Wikipedia lists 1967 as the last Fire Goat year, and the next will be 2087. Our five elements guide explains how the elements turn with the years.

The Goat is usually described as gentle, creative and caring. Put together with Yin Fire, a soft and steady fire rather than a blaze, the year is usually read as warmer and calmer than the fast Fire Horse year of 2026.

How each sign is read in a Goat year

The notes below use the same rules as this site's calculators, so they match what the compatibility tools and daily fortunes say:

  • Rabbit and Pig share the Goat's triangle (Rabbit, Goat and Pig) and are traditionally favoured.
  • Horse is listed in our guide as one of the Goat's supportive matches.
  • Ox sits directly opposite the Goat, the year's clash. Our guide to zodiac clashes explains what that means.
  • Goat people are in their own year, ben ming nian.
  • Everyone else is neutral: no special traditional relationship with the year.

These are cultural readings, not forecasts. There is no evidence that a year's animal decides anyone's luck; the value of the notes is as a prompt to think about your own strengths and habits. Each sign's lucky colors and numbers come from our animal guides.

Rat in 2027: neutral

The Rat is neutral to the Goat year in this site's system. A gentle, slower year can test the Rat's restlessness: opportunities are more likely to come through people than through quick deals. The Rat's habit of being careful with money and trust is an asset here; use the calmer pace to finish plans you started in the fast Horse year.

Ox in 2027: clash with the goat

The Ox sits directly opposite the Goat, so 2027 is its clash year. Tradition reads a clash as friction and change rather than misfortune: plans may need reshaping, and the Ox's stubborn streak can meet a year that rewards flexibility. A practical reading is to leave room in your schedule, check agreements twice, and let small disagreements go before they harden.

Tiger in 2027: neutral

The Tiger is neutral to the Goat year. After a lively Horse year that suited the Tiger's boldness, 2027 is quieter and more about people than speed. The Tiger's usual watch-out, impatience with slower people, is the thing to manage: a Goat year tends to reward those who bring others along.

Rabbit in 2027: same triangle as the goat

The Rabbit shares the Goat's triangle (Rabbit, Goat and Pig), so 2027 is traditionally one of its friendlier years. A calm, creative year suits the Rabbit's love of harmony. The watch-out is the Rabbit's habit of avoiding hard conversations; a supportive year is a good time to say what needs saying, gently.

Dragon in 2027: neutral

The Dragon is neutral to the Goat year. Big ambitions still have room, but a Goat year tends to favour care and cooperation over force. The Dragon's high standards can feel like impatience to others; sharing credit and slowing down to listen will help plans land.

Snake in 2027: neutral

The Snake is neutral to the Goat year. The Snake's careful planning fits a steady year well. The risk is keeping plans too private: in a year that favours warmth and cooperation, letting trusted people in early can open doors the Snake would not find alone.

Horse in 2027: supportive match

The Horse is in its own year until 5 February 2027, then hands over to the Goat. In this site's guide the Goat lists the Horse as one of its supportive matches, so 2027 is traditionally a friendly year for the Horse after the intensity of its own year. It is a good time to turn last year's energy into something lasting, rather than starting yet another new thing.

Goat in 2027: your own year

2027 is the Goat's own year, called ben ming nian. Tradition treats your own animal's year with some caution: China Highlights explains the belief that people in their zodiac year offend Tai Sui, and describes customs such as wearing red, ideally a gift from family or friends, and jade. Read it as a cultural custom, not a warning. A practical reading is to build on the Goat's creativity and care, and to ask for support rather than carrying everything alone.

Dog in 2027: neutral

Pig in 2027: same triangle as the goat

The Pig shares the Goat's triangle (Rabbit, Goat and Pig), so 2027 is traditionally one of its friendlier years. A warm, sociable year suits the Pig's generous nature. The watch-out is over-trusting or over-spending; enjoy the good company, and keep an eye on the budget.

Born in early 2027? Check the date

Because the zodiac year changes at Lunar New Year, not on 1 January, a baby born between 1 January and 5 February 2027 is still a Horse; a baby born from 6 February 2027 is a Goat. Our guide Born in January? explains the rule, and the zodiac sign checker applies the exact date for every year.

In Cambodia, the Khmer animal year changes at Khmer New Year in April instead. Our guide to Khmer New Year and the animal year explains the difference.

Frequently asked questions

When does the Year of the Goat 2027 start?

It starts with Chinese New Year on Saturday 6 February 2027 and ends on 25 January 2028.

What element is 2027?

Fire. 2027 is a Ding-wei year: Ding is the Yin Fire stem and Wei is the Goat branch, so it is called a Fire Goat year.

Which signs are favoured in 2027?

Traditionally the Rabbit and the Pig, which share the Goat's triangle, and the Horse, listed as a supportive match of the Goat.

Which sign clashes with the Goat year?

The Ox, which sits directly opposite the Goat on the zodiac circle.

Is your own zodiac year unlucky?

Tradition treats your own year (ben ming nian) with caution and suggests customs such as wearing red. It is a cultural custom, not a proven effect on luck.
