1961 Chinese Zodiac: Year of the Metal Ox

Born in 1961? Your Chinese zodiac animal is most likely the Ox, but check the date: the 1961 Metal Ox year began on 15 February 1961. Here is everything about the year, from its element to its lucky colors and best matches.

1961: a Yin Metal Ox year

The Chinese zodiac year 1961 was the Year of the Ox. It began with Lunar New Year on 15 February 1961 and ended on 4 February 1962, the eve of the next Lunar New Year.

Each year also has an element and a Yin or Yang quality. 1961 ends in 1, which makes it a Metal year, and as an odd year it is Yin. In the traditional stem-and-branch count, 1961 is a Xin-chou year. The same Metal Ox combination comes round only once every 60 years: 1901 before it and 2021 after it.

Born in January or February 1961?

Lunar New Year 1961 fell on 15 February 1961, so everyone born in January 1961, and in February before that date, belongs to the previous year's animal, the Rat.

The zodiac year starts at Lunar New Year, not on 1 January, which is why early-year birthdays need checking. Our guide Born in January? explains the rule, and the zodiac sign checker gives the exact answer for any birth date.

The Ox's personality

Our Ox guide describes the sign like this: “Reliable, honest, and hard-working. Oxen build success slowly and steadily through persistence.” The usual watch-out: “Can be stubborn and slow to adapt to sudden change.”

The Metal element adds its own flavour. Metal years bring discipline, ambition, and strong will. People of this element tend to be determined, independent, and value-driven. Read more in our Metal element guide, or the full Year of the Ox page.

Careers often linked with the Ox: agriculture, engineering, finance, medicine.

Lucky colors, numbers and days for the Ox

  • Lucky colors: white, yellow and green
  • Lucky numbers: 1 and 4
  • Lucky days: Wednesday and Friday

Best matches for someone born in 1961

In traditional compatibility, the Ox's best matches are the Rat, Snake and Rooster, and its clash sign is the Goat, which sits directly opposite it on the zodiac circle. Our compatibility calculator compares two full birth dates, elements included.

The Ox in 2026 and 2027

2026 (Fire Horse, until 5 February 2027): for the Ox, it has no special traditional relationship with the Horse, so 2026 is a neutral year.

2027 (Fire Goat, from 6 February 2027): for the Ox, it sits directly opposite the Goat, so 2027 is its clash year. See Chinese zodiac 2027 predictions for every sign.
