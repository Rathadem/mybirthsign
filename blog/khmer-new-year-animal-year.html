Khmer New Year: When the Animal Year Really Changes

✦ and why it is a different day from Chinese New Year ✦

Cambodia and China use the same twelve animals, but they start the new animal year at different times. Here is when each one changes, and what it means for your birthday.

Published October 17, 2026 · MyBirthSign

Ask a Cambodian grandmother which animal year you were born in, then check a Chinese zodiac website, and sometimes you get two different answers. Neither of them is wrong. Cambodia and China share the same twelve animals, from the Rat to the Pig, but each calendar starts the new animal year on its own New Year — and those two New Years are about two months apart.

This guide explains exactly when the animal year turns in the Khmer calendar, how that differs from Chinese New Year, and how to tell which animal you are in each system.

Two New Years, two different dates

Late January to mid-February

Chinese New Year

Chinese New Year follows the moon. It falls on the second new moon after the winter solstice, so the date moves every year, always between about 21 January and 20 February. The Chinese zodiac animal changes on that day.

13 or 14 April

Khmer New Year

Khmer New Year follows the sun. It begins at Moha Songkran, when the sun moves into the sign of Aries (Mesa) in the traditional Khmer reckoning. That is why it almost always starts on 13 or 14 April. The Khmer animal year changes at this moment, not in January or February.

The three days of Khmer New Year

  1. Day 1

    Moha Songkran

    The new year begins at an exact hour worked out by traditional astrologers. Families welcome the new Tevoda, the guardian angel who looks after the coming year, with offerings at home.

  2. Day 2

    Virak Vanabat

    A day for giving: gifts for parents and grandparents, food for monks, and help for people in need. In some years there are two Vanabat days, so the holiday lasts four days.

  3. Day 3

    Virak Loeng Sak

    The last day, when the year's Sak — its place in a ten-year cycle — moves up by one. Families bathe Buddha statues and pour scented water over the hands of their elders to ask for blessings.

So when exactly does the animal year change?

In the Khmer calendar, the new animal year starts at the moment of Moha Songkran on the first day of Khmer New Year. Because that moment can fall at any hour of the day, printed Khmer calendars usually show the new animal from the second day.

Two things change during the New Year days, and they are easy to mix up. The animal (for example Chnam Momee, the Year of the Horse) changes at Moha Songkran. The Sak (for example Atthasak) changes on the last day, Loeng Sak. A full Khmer year name combines both: Chnam Momee Atthasak.

If you were born on Moha Songkran itself, your Khmer animal depends on the time of birth: before the New Year hour you belong to the old animal, after it to the new one.

Which one should you use?

For Chinese zodiac readings — personality, compatibility, lucky colours and elements, as on this website — use the Chinese New Year rule. Our Zodiac Checker does this for you.

For Khmer traditions — the year name elders use, ceremonies at the pagoda, and readings by a Khmer astrologer (hora) — the Khmer calendar is the one that counts, and its animal changes at Khmer New Year.

Many Cambodian families use both without any problem. If your two animals differ, it simply means you were born in the weeks between the two New Years.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the Khmer animal year change on 1 January?

No. It changes at Khmer New Year in mid-April, at the moment of Moha Songkran. The Gregorian year and the Khmer animal year do not start on the same day.

Why is Khmer New Year in April when Chinese New Year is in January or February?

Chinese New Year is set by the moon (the second new moon after the winter solstice). Khmer New Year is set by the sun entering Aries in the traditional Khmer reckoning, which happens in mid-April.

I was born in March. Which animal am I?

In the Chinese zodiac you belong to the animal of that calendar year if you were born after Chinese New Year. In the Khmer calendar you still belong to the previous year's animal, because Khmer New Year has not happened yet.

Is the Khmer zodiac the same as the Chinese zodiac?

They use the same twelve animals in the same order, and the Khmer names (Choot, Chlov, Khal … Kor) match the Chinese ones. The difference is when each new year starts, and the Khmer calendar adds a ten-year Sak cycle.

高棉文化与历法

高棉新年：生肖年究竟何时更换

✦ 以及为什么它和农历新年不是同一天 ✦

柬埔寨和中国使用同样的十二生肖，但开始新生肖年的时间不同。下面说明两者各在何时更换，以及这对你的生日意味着什么。

发布于 2026年10月17日 · MyBirthSign

问柬埔寨的奶奶你属什么，再去查中国生肖网站，有时会得到两个不同的答案。两个答案都没有错。柬埔寨和中国共用从鼠到猪的十二生肖，但各自的历法在自己的新年开始新的生肖年——而这两个新年相隔大约两个月。

本指南说明高棉历中生肖年究竟何时更换、与农历新年有何不同，以及如何判断你在两种体系中各属什么。

两个新年，两个不同的日期

1月下旬至2月中旬

农历新年

农历新年依据月亮。它落在冬至后的第二个新月，所以日期每年不同，总在大约1月21日至2月20日之间。中国生肖在这一天更换。

4月13日或14日

高棉新年

高棉新年依据太阳。它从“大宋干”（Moha Songkran）开始，即按照高棉传统推算，太阳进入白羊宫（Mesa）的时刻。因此它几乎总是在4月13日或14日开始。高棉生肖年在这一刻更换，而不是在1月或2月。

高棉新年的三天

  1. 第1天

    大宋干（មហាសង្ក្រាន្ត）

    新年在传统星相师推算出的确切时刻开始。家家户户在家中摆设供品，迎接守护来年的新年天神（Tevoda）。

  2. 第2天

    万那巴日（វិរៈវ័នបត）

    布施的日子：给父母和祖父母送礼，为僧侣供斋，帮助有需要的人。有些年份有两天万那巴日，所以假期共四天。

  3. 第3天

    升年日（វិរៈឡើងស័ក）

    最后一天，这一年的“萨”（Sak，十年周期中的序数）加一。家人为佛像沐浴，并把香水洒在长辈手上，祈求祝福。

那么生肖年究竟何时更换？

在高棉历中，新的生肖年从新年第一天“大宋干”的那一刻开始。因为这个时刻可能落在一天中的任何时间，印刷的高棉日历通常从第二天起标注新的生肖。

新年期间有两样东西会变，很容易混淆。生肖（例如 ឆ្នាំមមី，马年）在大宋干时更换。萨（例如 អដ្ឋស័ក，Atthasak）在最后一天升年日更换。完整的高棉年名包含两者：ឆ្នាំមមី អដ្ឋស័ក。

如果你正好出生在大宋干当天，你的高棉生肖取决于出生时间：在新年时刻之前属旧生肖，之后属新生肖。

常见问题

高棉生肖年是在1月1日更换的吗？

不是。它在4月中旬高棉新年的大宋干时刻更换。公历年和高棉生肖年并不在同一天开始。

为什么高棉新年在4月，而农历新年在1月或2月？

农历新年由月亮决定（冬至后的第二个新月）。高棉新年由太阳进入白羊宫决定（高棉传统推算），这发生在4月中旬。

我出生在3月，属什么？

在中国生肖里，如果你出生在农历新年之后，就属当年的生肖。在高棉历里，你仍属上一年的生肖，因为高棉新年还没到。

高棉生肖和中国生肖一样吗？

两者使用同样的十二生肖、同样的顺序，高棉名称（ជូត、ឆ្លូវ、ខាល……កុរ）与中国名称一一对应。不同之处在于新年开始的时间，而且高棉历还多了一个十年的“萨”周期。
