    How Does the Zodiac Work?

    The zodiac is a repeating twelve-year cycle in which each year is represented by an animal: Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Goat, Monkey, Rooster, Dog and Pig. Tradition says your animal reflects temperament and outlook, and the same cycle is celebrated across China, Vietnam, Korea and Cambodia, where many families mark the lunar new year with food, family visits and temple blessings.

    Crucially, the cycle follows the lunar calendar, not January 1. Each zodiac year begins on the Lunar New Year, a date that moves between late January and mid-February. That is why this checker compares your birthday with the actual Lunar New Year date for your birth year instead of simply using the calendar year.

    The Five Zodiac Elements

    Alongside the twelve animals, each year carries one of five elements. Every element covers two consecutive years, so the full animal-and-element pattern repeats every sixty years.

    • Wood – growth, creativity and generosity.
    • Fire – passion, energy and enthusiasm.
    • Earth – stability, patience and practicality.
    • Metal – determination, discipline and clarity.
    • Water – adaptability, intuition and communication.

    Zodiac Personality Traits

    Traditional interpretations associate each animal with a set of characteristics. Horses are said to love freedom, Oxen to be dependable, and Dragons to be bold. These are cultural archetypes that many people enjoy as prompts for self-reflection, not fixed rules about who you are. Your element and Yin/Yang polarity add further nuance to the description.

    Zodiac Years

    The zodiac year does not always begin on 1 January. It starts on the Lunar New Year, so anyone born in January or early February may belong to the previous animal's year.     Frequently Asked Questions

    What is my zodiac sign?

    Your zodiac sign is the animal linked to your zodiac year, which runs from one Lunar New Year to the next. Enter your date of birth in the checker above and it will show your animal, element and Yin/Yang polarity.

    How do I find my zodiac animal?

    The twelve animals repeat in a fixed cycle: Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Goat, Monkey, Rooster, Dog and Pig. The cycle is tied to the zodiac year, not the calendar year, so the easiest way to be sure is to use a checker that looks up the real Lunar New Year date for your birth year.

    Why does my zodiac sign change if I was born in January or February?

    The zodiac year begins on the Lunar New Year, which falls between about 21 January and 20 February. If your birthday comes before that date, you belong to the previous zodiac year and its animal. For example, someone born on 10 January 1990 is a Snake, not a Horse.

    What are the five zodiac elements?

    The five elements are Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal and Water. Each element covers two consecutive years, and the pattern repeats every ten years. Combined with the twelve animals, they form a 60-year cycle, so a Wood Rat and a Water Rat are described a little differently.

    What does Yin and Yang mean in the zodiac?

    Yin and Yang are two complementary polarities in Chinese philosophy. In the zodiac, the animals alternate between them: Rat, Tiger, Dragon, Horse, Monkey and Dog are traditionally Yang (active, outward), while Ox, Rabbit, Snake, Goat, Rooster and Pig are Yin (receptive, inward).

    What is my zodiac element?

    Your element depends on the last digit of your zodiac year: 0 and 1 are Metal, 2 and 3 are Water, 4 and 5 are Wood, 6 and 7 are Fire, and 8 and 9 are Earth. The checker works this out for you automatically.

    Can I use my zodiac sign for compatibility?

    Many people enjoy comparing signs with a partner, friend or business partner. Traditional lore groups the animals into friendly triangles and notes a few challenging pairings, but it is a cultural tradition, not a guarantee. Try the compatibility checker for a fun, reflective comparison.

    Is zodiac astrology scientifically proven?

    No. The zodiac is a traditional cultural system, and there is no scientific evidence that it predicts personality or the future. We present it for entertainment, cultural exploration and personal reflection.

    生肖是怎么算的？

    生肖是一个每 12 年循环一次的周期，每一年由一种动物代表：鼠、牛、虎、兔、龙、蛇、马、羊、猴、鸡、狗、猪。传统认为，你的生肖反映了你的性情和人生态度。中国、越南、韩国和柬埔寨都庆祝同样的周期，许多家庭会在农历新年用美食、探亲和到寺庙祈福来迎接新的一年。

    最重要的是，这个周期按照农历，而不是从 1 月 1 日开始。每个生肖年都从农历新年开始，日期在 1 月下旬到 2 月中旬之间变动。所以本工具会把你的生日与你出生那年真实的农历新年日期作比较，而不是只看公历年份。

    生肖的五行

    除了十二生肖，每一年还对应五行之一。每种五行管两年，所以生肖与五行的完整组合每 60 年循环一次。

    • 木 – 成长、创意和慷慨。
    • 火 – 热情、活力和干劲。
    • 土 – 稳定、耐心和务实。
    • 金 – 决心、自律和清晰。
    • 水 – 适应力、直觉和沟通。

    生肖性格特点

    传统解读把每个生肖与一组性格特点联系在一起。人们说属马的人热爱自由，属牛的人可靠，属龙的人大胆。这些是文化上的原型，很多人把它们当作自我反思的提示，而不是关于你是谁的固定规则。你的五行和阴阳属性会为描述再增添一些细节。

    生肖配对

    传统上把十二生肖分成四个友好的“三合”组，并指出几组较难相处的配对。很多人在考虑伴侣、朋友或合作伙伴时，会把它当作聊天的话题。想比较两个生肖吗？试试我们的生肖配对查询，或看看商业合作配对。

    生肖年份

    生肖年并不总是从 1 月 1 日开始，而是从农历新年开始，所以 1 月或 2 月初出生的人可能属于上一年的生肖。浏览生肖指南查看每个生肖最近的年份，或阅读逐年指南，例如马年。

    常见问题

    我属什么生肖？

    你的生肖是你所在生肖年对应的动物，生肖年从一个农历新年到下一个农历新年。在上方输入你的出生日期，就会显示你的生肖、五行和阴阳属性。

    怎样找到自己的生肖？

    十二生肖按固定顺序循环：鼠、牛、虎、兔、龙、蛇、马、羊、猴、鸡、狗、猪。这个周期跟生肖年走，而不是公历年，所以最稳妥的办法是使用会查询你出生年份真实农历新年日期的工具。

    为什么 1 月或 2 月出生的人生肖会不一样？

    生肖年从农历新年开始，农历新年大约在 1 月 21 日到 2 月 20 日之间。如果你的生日在那一天之前，你就属于上一个生肖年和它的动物。例如，1990 年 1 月 10 日出生的人属蛇，而不是属马。

    生肖的五行是什么？

    五行是木、火、土、金、水。每种五行管连续两年，每 10 年循环一次。与十二生肖组合在一起，就形成 60 年的周期，所以木鼠和水鼠的描述会有一些不同。

    生肖中的阴阳是什么意思？

    阴和阳是中国哲学中两个互补的属性。在生肖中，动物交替属阴或属阳：鼠、虎、龙、马、猴、狗传统上属阳（主动、外向），牛、兔、蛇、羊、鸡、猪属阴（包容、内敛）。

    我的五行是什么？

    你的五行取决于生肖年份的最后一位数字：0 和 1 属金，2 和 3 属水，4 和 5 属木，6 和 7 属火，8 和 9 属土。本工具会自动为你算出来。

    可以用生肖看配对吗？

    很多人喜欢和伴侣、朋友或合作伙伴比较生肖。传统上把动物分成友好的三合组，并指出几组较难相处的配对，但这是一种文化传统，而不是保证。试试配对查询，来一次有趣、引人思考的比较。

    生肖命理有科学依据吗？
