Cambodian Traditions

Lucky and Unlucky Days to Open a Shop or Business

✦ How Cambodian families choose an opening day ✦

From asking an achar to checking the owner's zodiac animal, here is how a good opening day is traditionally chosen — plus a tool that finds the favourable days for your sign this month.

Published October 17, 2026 · By Ratha, MyBirthSign

In Cambodia, opening a new shop, restaurant or office is rarely done on just any day. Many families believe the first day sets the tone for the business, so they take care to start on a day that feels lucky — and to avoid one that does not.

There is no single rulebook. Different families, regions and communities follow different customs, and many combine more than one. This guide explains the most common ways a good opening day is chosen, what is traditionally avoided, and how you can check the days that suit your own zodiac animal.

Four ways families choose a good day

  1. 1

    Ask an achar or a hora

    The most traditional way is to ask a respected achar (lay ceremony master at the pagoda) or a hora (Khmer astrologer). They usually ask for the owner's birth date and sometimes the birth time, then suggest a day and an auspicious hour to open the doors.

  2. 2

    Look at the Khmer lunar calendar

    Many people like to begin new things while the moon is waxing (Kaet), because the growing moon stands for growth and increase. Rules differ between families, so the waxing half is a preference rather than a fixed law.

  3. 3

    Match the day to the owner's zodiac animal

    Many Chinese-Cambodian families, and many others too, check the Chinese almanac. Every day has its own animal in a repeating 12-day cycle. A day whose animal is a friend of the owner's sign is favourable; a day that clashes with it is avoided.

  4. 4

    Think about customers too

    Tradition works best alongside common sense: a day when customers can come, a date after payday, good weather for an outdoor stall, and enough time to have everything ready.

Which days are lucky — and which are not

Favourable: a day whose animal is in the owner's triangle of friends (for example Rat, Dragon and Monkey), or the owner's “secret friend” sign (the six-harmony pair, such as Rat and Ox).

Avoid: a day whose animal is directly opposite the owner's sign on the zodiac wheel — the clash day. For a Rat owner that is a Horse day; for a Tiger owner, a Monkey day.

Neutral: every other day. It is not unlucky; it just has no special support.

If two partners open the business together, families often look for a day that suits both, or at least one that clashes with neither.

A typical opening ceremony

  1. 1

    Prepare

    The shop is cleaned and decorated, often with flowers, banana-leaf offerings and fruit. Many families set up a small shrine for the spirit of the land.

  2. 2

    Bless

    Monks are invited to chant blessings in the morning and are offered food. Holy water is sprinkled over the shop, the owners and the goods.

  3. 3

    Open

    At the chosen hour the doors open. Owners often make the first sale to a family member or a friend whose sign suits the business, for good luck.

Customs differ from family to family; some keep the ceremony small and private, others invite neighbours and customers.

Lucky numbers and colours

Some owners also choose a shop number, a sign colour or an opening date that matches the lucky numbers and colours of their zodiac animal. You can find yours on the Zodiac Checker or your animal's page.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the luckiest day to open a business in Cambodia?

There is no single luckiest day for everyone. Traditionally it depends on the owner: a day whose animal is in the owner's triangle of friends, or the owner's secret-friend sign, is favourable, and many people also prefer the waxing moon. An achar or hora can suggest a specific day and hour.

Which day should I avoid?

The day whose animal clashes with the owner's sign — the sign directly opposite on the zodiac wheel. For example, Rat owners traditionally avoid Horse days.

Do I need an achar?

No. Many families do ask one, especially for a big opening, but plenty of owners simply check the zodiac days and the lunar calendar themselves.

What if two partners have different signs?

Look for a day that is favourable for at least one partner and clashes with neither. Our Business Partner tool also compares the two signs.

柬埔寨传统

开店或创业的吉日与忌日

✦ 柬埔寨家庭如何选择开业日 ✦

从请教阿佳（achar）到查看店主的生肖，下面介绍传统上如何挑选开业吉日——另附一个工具，帮你找出本月适合你生肖的日子。

发布于 2026年10月17日 · 作者 Ratha, MyBirthSign

在柬埔寨，开一家新店、餐馆或办公室，很少随便挑一天。许多家庭相信第一天会为生意定下基调，所以会小心地选一个吉利的日子开始——并避开不吉利的日子。

这并没有统一的规则。不同的家庭、地区和社区遵循不同的习俗，很多人还会把几种方法结合起来。本指南介绍最常见的选日方法、传统上要避开什么，以及如何查看适合你自己生肖的日子。

家庭选择吉日的四种方法

  1. 1

    请教阿佳或星相师

    最传统的方法是请一位受人尊敬的阿佳（achar，寺庙里主持仪式的居士）或星相师（hora）。他们通常会问店主的出生日期，有时还问出生时间，然后建议一个开门营业的日子和吉时。

  2. 2

    看高棉农历

    许多人喜欢在月亮渐盈（上弦，Kaet）时开始新的事情，因为逐渐变圆的月亮象征成长和增益。各家规矩不同，所以上弦只是一种偏好，并非硬性规定。

  3. 3

    把日子和店主的生肖对照

    许多柬埔寨华裔家庭，以及其他很多人，会查看中国的通书（黄历）。每一天在12天的循环中都有自己的生肖。当天的生肖与店主生肖相合就是吉日；与之相冲的日子则要避开。

  4. 4

    也要考虑顾客

    传统最好和常识一起用：选顾客方便来的日子、发薪日之后的日期、适合户外摊位的好天气，以及足够的准备时间。

哪些日子吉利，哪些不吉利

吉：当天的生肖与店主同属三合（例如鼠、龙、猴），或是店主的“暗中贵人”生肖（六合，例如鼠与牛）。

忌：当天的生肖在生肖轮盘上与店主生肖正好相对——即相冲日。属鼠的店主忌马日；属虎的店主忌猴日。

平：其他所有日子。并非不吉，只是没有特别的助力。

如果两位合伙人一起开业，家人通常会找一个对两人都合适的日子，或至少不与任何一人相冲的日子。

典型的开业仪式

  1. 1

    准备

    把店铺打扫干净并装饰，常用鲜花、蕉叶供品和水果。许多家庭会设一个小神龛供奉土地神。

  2. 2

    祈福

    早上请僧侣来诵经祈福，并供养斋饭。在店铺、店主和货物上洒圣水。

  3. 3

    开门

    在选定的时刻打开店门。店主常把第一笔生意卖给生肖与生意相合的亲友，以求好运。

习俗因家庭而异：有的仪式简单私密，有的会邀请邻居和顾客。

常见问题

在柬埔寨开业最吉利的日子是哪天？

并没有一个对所有人都最吉利的日子。传统上取决于店主：当天生肖与店主同属三合，或是店主的六合生肖，就是吉日；许多人也偏爱上弦月的日子。阿佳或星相师可以建议具体的日子和时辰。

应该避开哪一天？

当天生肖与店主生肖相冲的日子——即在生肖轮盘上正对的生肖。例如，属鼠的店主传统上避开马日。

一定要请阿佳吗？

不一定。许多家庭会请，尤其是大型开业，但也有很多店主只是自己查看生肖日和农历。

如果两位合伙人生肖不同怎么办？

找一个至少对一位合伙人吉利、且不与任何一人相冲的日子。我们的商业伙伴工具也能比较两个生肖。
