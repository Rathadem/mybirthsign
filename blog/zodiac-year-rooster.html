🐓 Rooster Chinese Zodiac: Personality, Compatibility & Today's Fortune

Ad space

If you were born in one of these years, you belong to the Rooster: 1969, 1981, 1993, 2005, 2017, 2029, 2041 (the cycle repeats every 12 years, so the pattern continues both before and after).

The Rooster takes real pride in doing things properly, down to the smallest detail most people would never think to check. This makes Roosters excellent at any work that rewards precision and high standards — the kind of person a team relies on to catch the mistake everyone else missed. That same eye for flaws can turn inward or outward as criticism, so Roosters do well to remember that not every imperfection needs to be pointed out.

Personality

Observant, hard-working, and confident. Roosters are detail-oriented and take pride in doing things well.

Roosters have a genuine gift for noticing the one detail everyone else overlooked, and they take real pride in work that holds up to close scrutiny. That same sharp eye is always on, though, which means a Rooster rarely lets a flaw pass without comment — even when no one asked for the feedback.

In relationships

Roosters are devoted, high-standards partners who show love by actually doing things — fixing, planning, taking care of the details that keep a relationship running smoothly. They do best with a partner who can hear their directness as care rather than criticism, and who'll say so plainly when it starts to feel like nagging.

At work

Roosters are the person a team trusts to catch the mistake before it ships — meticulous, well-prepared, and genuinely proud of doing things properly. They can rub colleagues the wrong way when that same standard gets applied out loud to other people's work, so a little tact goes a long way.

What to watch for

Can be overly critical or blunt with others. Not every flaw needs pointing out in the moment, and a Rooster who learns to save some observations for later tends to keep relationships smoother without losing their standards.
