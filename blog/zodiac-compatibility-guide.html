Zodiac Compatibility Guide

✦ Your best matches in love, friendship and business ✦

How the 12 Chinese zodiac animals connect: which signs are most compatible, which bring balance, and which may need more patience.

Updated October 11, 2026 · MyBirthSign

One of the most popular reasons people look up their Chinese zodiac sign is to check compatibility — with a partner, a friend or a business partner. Traditional zodiac compatibility is built on a few simple ideas: “triangles” of signs that naturally support each other, special “six harmony” pairs, and direct “clashes” between signs that sit opposite each other on the zodiac wheel.

Compatibility Groups

The four triangles (best match)

The 12 animals form four groups of three. Signs in the same group share a similar pace and outlook, and often feel comfortable together straight away.

  • Rat · Dragon · MonkeyAmbitious, quick-thinking, action-oriented
  • Ox · Snake · RoosterPatient, detail-focused, hard-working
  • Tiger · Horse · DogIndependent, loyal, energetic
  • Rabbit · Goat · PigGentle, artistic, relationship-focused

Each animal also has one “secret friend” (六合, six harmony): a single sign that brings it special support.

Each animal has one clash partner, directly opposite it on the 12-year wheel. A clash does not mean a relationship is doomed — only that the two signs see life differently enough to need extra communication and patience.

Relationship Guidance

  • Love & romance

    A best-match or good-match sign can make a relationship feel easy. With a challenging sign, talk early about money, plans and how you each like to spend time.

  • Friendship

    Friends in the same triangle often “get” each other at once. Neutral signs can become lifelong friends through shared interests and time together.

  • Business partnership

    Opposite signs can make strong partners because they notice what the other misses. Divide roles clearly and check in often. Check a business partner →

  • Balance & understanding

    Every pair can work well with respect, patience and open communication. Your element, upbringing and choices matter as much as your animal.

Compatibility Isn’t Destiny

Two people from “clashing” signs can have wonderful relationships, and two people from the same triangle can still struggle if other parts of the relationship are not working. Use zodiac compatibility as a conversation starter and a bit of fun insight — not as a reason to avoid getting to know someone.

十二生肖之间如何相处：哪些生肖最相配，哪些能带来平衡，哪些可能需要更多耐心。

更新于 2026年10月11日 · MyBirthSign

人们查询生肖最常见的原因之一，就是看看配不配——和伴侣、朋友或生意伙伴。传统的生肖配对建立在几个简单的概念上：彼此天然扶持的“三合”、特别的“六合”组合，以及在生肖轮盘上彼此正对的“相冲”。

配对类型

  • 最佳配对同属三合：天然和谐，联系紧密。
  • 良好配对六合：互相扶持的正面关系。
  • 普通彼此理解就能相处得很好。
  • 有挑战相冲生肖：可能面临差异和冲突。
  • 同一生肖容易理解彼此，但相似的长处会互相竞争。

配对组合

四组三合（最佳配对）

十二生肖分成四组，每组三个。同组的生肖节奏和想法相近，常常一见面就觉得自在。

  • 鼠 · 龙 · 猴有抱负、思维敏捷、注重行动
  • 牛 · 蛇 · 鸡有耐心、注重细节、勤奋
  • 虎 · 马 · 狗独立、忠诚、精力充沛
  • 兔 · 羊 · 猪温和、有艺术气质、重视感情

六合（良好配对）

每个生肖还有一个“秘密朋友”（六合）：一个能给它特别支持的生肖。

每个生肖都有一个相冲的生肖，在十二年的轮盘上与它正对。相冲并不意味着关系注定失败——只是两个生肖看待生活的方式差异较大，需要更多沟通和耐心。

关系建议

  • 爱情与浪漫

    最佳或良好配对的生肖会让感情显得轻松。如果是有挑战的生肖，尽早聊聊金钱、计划，以及各自喜欢怎样度过时间。

  • 友情

    同属三合的朋友常常一下子就懂彼此。普通配对的生肖也能通过共同兴趣和相处成为一生的朋友。

  • 生意合作

    相冲的生肖也能成为强大的合作伙伴，因为他们能注意到对方忽略的地方。明确分工，经常沟通。查看商业伙伴配对 →

  • 平衡与理解

    只要彼此尊重、有耐心、坦诚沟通，任何组合都能相处得很好。你的五行、成长环境和选择与生肖同样重要。

配对不是命运

生肖“相冲”的两个人也可以拥有美好的关系，而同属三合的两个人，如果关系中其他方面出了问题，也可能过得很辛苦。把生肖配对当作聊天的话题和一点有趣的参考——而不是拒绝去了解一个人的理由。
