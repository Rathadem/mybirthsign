Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MyBirthSign ("we", "our", "the site") handles information when you visit mybirthsign.com.

Information We Collect

We do not create user accounts or keep visitor profiles. The zodiac checker, compatibility and other calculators run entirely in your browser — the birth dates you enter are not sent to or stored on our servers (on the compatibility page they may appear in your own browser's address bar so you can reload the result; the share buttons never include them). We do not currently use a separate analytics tool. Like every website, our hosting provider, Netlify, processes technical request data (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) to deliver and protect the site.

Cookies and Advertising

This site may use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners may use cookies, device identifiers, or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses data and manage your ad personalization settings at Google's Ads Policy page.

AI Friend Chat

MyBirthSign AI Friend (Kru Toch) is an optional chat assistant. It is an AI, not a person. When you use it, the messages you type (and any name or birth date you share in the chat) are sent securely from our server to Anthropic, the company that provides the Claude AI service, so that a reply can be generated. Please do not share sensitive personal information in the chat. We do not use the chat to identify you, and the conversation is kept only in your own browser for the current visit; it is cleared when you close the tab or press the refresh button in the chat. Zodiac results are traditional and cultural interpretations for entertainment, not professional advice. Anthropic handles data according to its own privacy policy.

Daily Fortune, Sharing and Language

The daily Chinese zodiac readings are prepared once a day by our own scheduled job (traditional rules plus wording written with Anthropic's Claude AI) and published as a public file; they contain no visitor information. When you use a Share button, the link contains only the zodiac animals, the date and your language — never names or birth dates. Shared and downloaded pictures are drawn on your own device and are not uploaded to or stored by us. The social network or app you choose (such as Facebook, WhatsApp, Telegram, Pinterest, Instagram or TikTok) receives what you share and handles it under its own privacy policy, and services like Facebook and Telegram may visit the shared link to show a preview. Your language choice (English or Khmer) is remembered in your own browser only. To prevent abuse of the chat, our server counts recent requests per IP address in temporary memory; this count is not saved.

Third-Party Links

This site may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those external sites.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via our contact page.
