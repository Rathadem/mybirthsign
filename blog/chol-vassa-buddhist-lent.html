Chol Vassa: Buddhist Lent in Cambodia

✦ the three-month rains retreat, its dates, and what it means for family plans ✦

Chol Vassa begins the three months when Buddhist monks stay in one monastery for the rainy season. In 2026 it began on Thursday 30 July 2026, and it ends at Chenh Vassa on Monday 26 October 2026.

Published October 17, 2026 · MyBirthSign

Every year, on the day after the full moon of the month of Asadh, monks across Cambodia begin Vassa, the rains retreat. For about three lunar months they stay at their own monastery to study, meditate and teach. Lay families visit the pagoda more often, and many quietly change their plans for the season.

This guide explains where the custom comes from, how its dates are worked out, what families in Cambodia traditionally do during these months, and why the start date moves so much from year to year. There is a table for 2024 to 2030 and a checker for any date.

Where the rains retreat comes from

In the Buddha's time, monks walked from village to village all year. Buddhist texts tell that during the rainy season people complained: wandering monks trampled young rice plants and small creatures in the wet fields. The Buddha then asked monks to stay in one place for the three months of the rains.

The rule is still followed. A monk who enters Vassa promises to spend the season at one monastery. He may leave for up to seven days for a real need, such as caring for a sick parent, but he must return before the seventh dawn. A monk's seniority is still counted in the number of Vassa seasons he has completed.

How the start and end dates are set

1st waning day of Asadh

Chol Vassa

The retreat begins on the day after the full moon of Asadh, the eighth Khmer lunar month. The full moon itself is Asalha Puja, which remembers the Buddha's first sermon. In years with a leap month there are two Asadh months, and Vassa begins in the second one (Tutiyasadh).

Full moon of Assoch

Chenh Vassa

The retreat ends three lunar months later, on the full moon of Assoch. That day is Chenh Vassa. From the next day until the full moon of Kadeuk is the Kathin season, when communities offer new robes to the monks.

Why the date jumps around

The Khmer lunar year is about 11 days shorter than the solar year, so Chol Vassa usually comes about 11 days earlier than the year before. To stop the months drifting away from the seasons, the calendar adds a leap month (Adhikameas) every two or three years by repeating Asadh.

That is why the dates swing: in 2025 Vassa began on 11 July 2025, but 2026 had two Asadh months, so it began almost three weeks later, on 30 July 2026. The next leap year in the table is 2029.

What families traditionally do

  1. Before the retreat

    Ordinations

    Many young men are ordained in the weeks before Chol Vassa, so they can spend the whole retreat as monks. For the family it is a merit-making event, often celebrated with relatives.

  2. At Chol Vassa

    Candles and rain cloths

    Families and villages offer large Vassa candles to light the temple through the season, and rain-bathing cloths for the monks. Some places carry the candles to the pagoda in a procession.

  3. During Vassa

    Precepts and Pchum Ben

    Many lay people keep the precepts more strictly on holy days, and some give up alcohol for the season. Pchum Ben, the 15 days for ancestors, falls inside Vassa, ending on the day shown in the table.

  4. After Chenh Vassa

    Kathin

    The month after the retreat is for Kathin: a community offers new robes and gifts to a temple in a procession. Each temple can receive the Kathin offering once a season.

Weddings and other plans

Many Cambodian families do not hold weddings during the three months of Vassa, and the wedding season traditionally picks up again after Chenh Vassa. The Buddhist rule binds the monks, not lay people, so this is a family custom rather than a religious law. How strictly it is kept differs between families and regions, so ask your elders or the achar who will lead the ceremony.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Chol Vassa in 2026?

Chol Vassa 2026 was on Thursday 30 July 2026. The retreat ends at Chenh Vassa on Monday 26 October 2026, and the Kathin season runs until 24 November 2026.

Why does Chol Vassa fall on a different date every year?

It follows the Khmer lunar calendar: it is always the day after the full moon of Asadh. Lunar months do not line up with the Gregorian calendar, and some years add a second Asadh, which moves Vassa about three weeks later.

How long does Vassa last?

Three lunar months, from the 1st waning day of Asadh to the full moon of Assoch. In 2026 that was 89 days, counting both the first and last day.

Can monks leave the temple during Vassa?

Only for a real need, such as a sick parent or an important duty, and for no more than seven days. Otherwise a monk spends the whole season at the monastery where he entered Vassa.

Can you get married during Vassa?

There is no Buddhist rule against lay weddings, but many Cambodian families avoid them during Vassa by custom and wait until after Chenh Vassa. Ask your family's elders or achar what they follow.

Is Pchum Ben part of Vassa?

Yes. Pchum Ben falls inside the retreat, at the end of the month of Phatrabot. Chenh Vassa comes about two weeks after Pchum Ben day.

高棉文化与历法

入雨安居：柬埔寨的佛教守夏节

✦ 为期三个月的雨季安居、它的日期，以及对家庭安排的影响 ✦

入雨安居（Chol Vassa）标志着僧人在雨季留在同一座寺院的三个月开始。2026年的入雨安居是2026年7月30日（星期四），出雨安居是2026年10月26日（星期一）。

发布于 2026年10月17日 · MyBirthSign

每年在阿沙荼月（Asadh）满月的第二天，柬埔寨各地的僧人开始“雨安居”（Vassa）。在大约三个农历月里，他们留在自己的寺院学习、禅修和讲法。居士家庭更常去寺院，许多人也会悄悄调整这一季的计划。

本文介绍这一习俗的由来、日期如何推算、柬埔寨家庭在这几个月里传统上会做什么，以及为什么开始日期每年变化这么大。文中附有2024至2030年的日期表，以及可查询任意日期的工具。

雨安居的由来

在佛陀的时代，僧人一年四季在村落之间游行。佛经记载，雨季时人们抱怨四处行走的僧人踩坏了湿田里的秧苗和小生物。于是佛陀要求僧人在雨季的三个月里留在一处。

这条戒律至今仍被遵守。进入安居的僧人发愿整季留在同一座寺院。若有真正的需要，例如照顾生病的父母，他可以离开最多七天，但必须在第七天黎明前回来。僧人的“戒腊”至今仍以完成的安居次数来计算。

开始和结束日期如何确定

阿沙荼月下弦第1天

入雨安居

安居在阿沙荼月（高棉第八个农历月）满月的第二天开始。满月当天是三宝佛节（Asalha Puja），纪念佛陀初转法轮。在有闰月的年份会有两个阿沙荼月，安居从第二个阿沙荼月（Tutiyasadh）开始。

阿说逝月满月

出雨安居

三个农历月后，在阿说逝月（Assoch）的满月日结束，这一天就是出雨安居（Chenh Vassa）。从第二天到迦提月（Kadeuk）满月是功德衣（Kathin）时节，信众在这段时间向僧人供养新袈裟。

为什么日期变化这么大

高棉农历年比阳历年短约11天，所以入雨安居通常比前一年早约11天。为了不让月份偏离季节，历法每两三年加一个闰月（Adhikameas），也就是重复一次阿沙荼月。

这就是日期摆动的原因：2025年的安居从2025年7月11日开始，而2026年有两个阿沙荼月，所以晚了将近三周，在2026年7月30日开始。表中下一个闰年是2029年。

家庭传统上会做什么

  1. 安居之前

    出家

    许多年轻男子在入雨安居前几周出家，以便整季以僧人身份安居。对家庭来说这是积功德的大事，常常和亲友一起庆祝。

  2. 入雨安居当天

    安居蜡烛与雨浴衣

    家庭和村落向寺院供养大蜡烛，让寺院整季都有灯光，也向僧人供养雨浴衣。有些地方会抬着蜡烛游行到寺院。

  3. 安居期间

    持戒与亡人节

    许多居士在斋戒日更严格地持戒，有些人整季戒酒。为祖先而设的15天亡人节（Pchum Ben）也在安居期间，结束于表中所示的日子。

  4. 出雨安居之后

    功德衣节

    安居结束后的一个月是功德衣（Kathin）时节：信众列队向寺院供养新袈裟和礼物。每座寺院每一季只能接受一次功德衣供养。

婚礼和其他安排

许多柬埔寨家庭在安居的三个月里不办婚礼，传统上婚礼旺季在出雨安居之后才重新开始。佛教戒律约束的是僧人而不是居士，所以这是家庭习俗，而不是宗教法规。遵守的严格程度因家庭和地区而异，请向长辈或主持仪式的阿匝（achar）请教。

常见问题

2026年入雨安居是哪一天？

2026年入雨安居是2026年7月30日（星期四），出雨安居是2026年10月26日（星期一），功德衣时节一直到2026年11月24日。

为什么入雨安居每年日期不同？

它按高棉农历计算：永远是阿沙荼月满月的第二天。农历月份与阳历不对应，而且有些年份会多一个阿沙荼月，使安居推迟约三周。

安居持续多久？

三个农历月，从阿沙荼月下弦第1天到阿说逝月满月。2026年共89天（首尾两天都计算在内）。

僧人在安居期间可以离开寺院吗？

只有在真正需要时才可以，例如父母生病或重要事务，而且不超过七天。除此之外，僧人整季都留在他入安居的寺院。

安居期间可以结婚吗？

佛教戒律并不禁止居士结婚，但许多柬埔寨家庭按习俗避开安居期，等到出雨安居之后。请向家中长辈或阿匝请教你们家遵循的做法。

亡人节是安居的一部分吗？

是的。亡人节在安居期间，位于帕陀罗钵陀月（Phatrabot）月底。出雨安居大约在亡人节正日两周之后。
