The Spirit of the Rooster

The Rooster traditionally represents diligence, honesty, and the pride of doing things well.

Understanding the Rooster

The Rooster takes real pride in doing things properly, down to the smallest detail most people would never think to check. This makes Roosters excellent at any work that rewards precision and high standards — the kind of person a team relies on to catch the mistake everyone else missed. That same eye for flaws can turn inward or outward as criticism, so Roosters do well to remember that not every imperfection needs to be pointed out.

Personality

Observant, hard-working, and confident. Roosters are detail-oriented and take pride in doing things well.

Roosters have a genuine gift for noticing the one detail everyone else overlooked, and they take real pride in work that holds up to close scrutiny. That same sharp eye is always on, though, which means a Rooster rarely lets a flaw pass without comment — even when no one asked for the feedback.

In relationships

Roosters are devoted, high-standards partners who show love by actually doing things — fixing, planning, taking care of the details that keep a relationship running smoothly. They do best with a partner who can hear their directness as care rather than criticism, and who'll say so plainly when it starts to feel like nagging.

At work

Roosters are the person a team trusts to catch the mistake before it ships — meticulous, well-prepared, and genuinely proud of doing things properly. They can rub colleagues the wrong way when that same standard gets applied out loud to other people's work, so a little tact goes a long way.

What to watch for

Can be overly critical or blunt with others. Not every flaw needs pointing out in the moment, and a Rooster who learns to save some observations for later tends to keep relationships smoother without losing their standards.

Compatibility

Roosters work well with equally disciplined, detail-minded signs, and clash most with easygoing signs who find their bluntness harder to take.

Best matches: Ox, Snake, Dragon

Can clash with: Rabbit

Careers that often fit well

Work that rewards precision, preparation, and high standards tends to be where a Rooster's instincts pay off most.

Accounting, journalism, quality control, public speaking

Frequently Asked Questions

What years are the Year of the Rooster?

Rooster years include 1969, 1981, 1993, 2005, 2017, 2029, 2041. The zodiac repeats every 12 years, and the zodiac year begins at Lunar New Year, not on January 1.

What personality traits are associated with the Rooster?

Traditionally, the Rooster is associated with courage, confidence, independence, and determination. Observant, hard-working, and confident. Roosters are detail-oriented and take pride in doing things well.

What are the lucky colors for the Rooster?

The Rooster's lucky colors in our guide are Gold, Brown, Yellow.

What are the lucky numbers for the Rooster?

The Rooster's lucky numbers are 5, 7, 8.

Which zodiac animals are compatible with the Rooster?

Best matches are Ox and Snake, which share the Rooster's compatibility triangle. Dragon is also listed as a supportive match in our guide, while Rabbit can be the most challenging pairing.

What element is my Rooster year?

It depends on your birth year — Wood: 2005 · Fire: 2017 · Earth: 1969, 2029 · Metal: 1981, 2041 · Water: 1993. Use the Zodiac Checker to confirm yours.

Does the Chinese zodiac year start on January 1?

No. The zodiac year begins at Lunar New Year, which falls between late January and mid-February. If you were born in January or February, check the Lunar New Year date or use our Zodiac Checker.

Is Chinese zodiac personality scientifically proven?

No. Chinese zodiac interpretations are traditional and cultural, and they are not scientifically proven. Enjoy them for fun and self-reflection.

ក្នុងទំនាក់ទំនងស្នេហា

តើឆ្នាំរកាគឺឆ្នាំអ្វីខ្លះ?

ឆ្នាំរការួមមាន 1969, 1981, 1993, 2005, 2017, 2029, 2041។ រង្វង់រាសីដដែលៗរៀងរាល់ ១២ឆ្នាំម្តង ហើយឆ្នាំរាសីចាប់ផ្តើមនៅថ្ងៃចូលឆ្នាំចន្ទគតិ មិនមែនថ្ងៃទី១ មករាទេ។

តើបុគ្គលិកលក្ខណៈអ្វីខ្លះដែលផ្សារភ្ជាប់នឹងរកា?

តើសត្វណាខ្លះត្រូវគ្នាជាមួយរកា?

ទេ។ ឆ្នាំរាសីចាប់ផ្តើមនៅថ្ងៃចូលឆ្នាំចន្ទគតិ ដែលធ្លាក់នៅចន្លោះចុងខែមករា និងពាក់កណ្តាលខែកុម្ភៈ។ ប្រសិនបើអ្នកកើតក្នុងខែទាំងពីរនេះ សូមពិនិត្យជាមួយម៉ាស៊ីនពិនិត្យរបស់យើង។
