The Five Elements Explained: Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, Water

Ad space

Most people know their zodiac animal, but far fewer know their element — even though it adds an important second layer to the picture. While the 12 animals repeat every 12 years, the five elements (Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, and Water) repeat on a 10-year cycle. Because 12 and 10 don't share a simple relationship, the two cycles only fully realign every 60 years, meaning a specific combination like "Earth Dragon" only comes around once every six decades.

Wood

Wood years are associated with growth, flexibility, and cooperation. People born under Wood tend to be generous, idealistic, and good at working within a team. The downside is a tendency to take on too much or struggle to say no.

Fire

Fire brings energy, passion, and boldness. Fire-element people are often natural leaders who act quickly and aren't afraid to take risks. The challenge is tempering that intensity so it doesn't burn out relationships or opportunities too fast.

Earth

Earth is the element of stability and practicality. People with this element tend to be dependable, patient, and skilled at long-term planning. They can sometimes be seen as overly cautious or slow to embrace sudden change.

Metal

Metal represents discipline, structure, and strong will. Metal-element people are often determined and focused once they set a goal, valuing honesty and clear principles. The trade-off can be rigidity or difficulty compromising.

Water

Water is linked to intuition, adaptability, and calm thinking. Water-element people are often diplomatic and perceptive, able to read a room and adjust easily. The risk is being too passive or avoiding decisions that need to be made.

How to Find Your Element

Your element is determined by the last digit of your birth year, following a repeating 10-year pattern: Wood, Wood, Fire, Fire, Earth, Earth, Metal, Metal, Water, Water. Rather than counting it out by hand, you can enter your birthday into the checker tool to see your animal and element together instantly.

ដី

ដីជាធាតុនៃស្ថេរភាព និងការអនុវត្តជាក់ស្តែង។ អ្នកមានធាតុនេះទុកចិត្តបាន អត់ធ្មត់ និងពូកែក្នុងការរៀបចំ ផែនការរយៈពេលវែង។ ជួនកាលពួកគេអាចមើលទៅប្រុងប្រយ័ត្នខ្លាំងពេក ឬយឺតក្នុងការទទួលយកការផ្លាស់ប្តូរភ្លាមៗ។

លោហធាតុ

លោហធាតុតំណាងឱ្យវិន័យ រចនាសម្ព័ន្ធ និងឆន្ទៈខ្លាំង។ អ្នកមានធាតុលោហៈជារឿយៗប្តេជ្ញា និងផ្តោតអារម្មណ៍ម្តងពួកគេ កំណត់គោលដៅ ដោយផ្តល់តម្លៃលើភាពស្មោះត្រង់ និងគោលការណ៍ច្បាស់លាស់។ ចំណុចខ្វះខាតអាចជាភាពរឹងរូស ឬពិបាកក្នុង ការសម្របសម្រួល។
