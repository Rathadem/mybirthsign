Born in January? Your Chinese Zodiac Sign Is Probably Last Year's Animal

If you were born in January, your Chinese zodiac animal is very likely not the one for your birth year. Here is the simple rule, the years when late-January babies are the exception, and how to check your own date.

Published October 27, 2026 · By Ratha, MyBirthSign

Born 21–31 JanuaryDepends on the year

The short answer

The Chinese zodiac year does not start on 1 January. It starts on Lunar New Year, which moves with the moon. The Farmers' Almanac explains that it usually falls on the second new moon after the winter solstice, between 21 January and 21 February. In the Lunar New Year table our zodiac checker uses, which covers 1900 to 2060, the earliest date is 21 January and the latest is 20 February.

That gives a simple rule:

  • Born 1–20 January: you always belong to the previous year's animal.
  • Born 21–31 January: it depends on the year. If Lunar New Year that year fell on or before your birthday, you take the new animal; otherwise the previous one.
  • Born in February: it depends on the year too, up to 20 February.

Two examples

Someone born on 15 January 2000 is a Rabbit, not a Dragon: Lunar New Year 2000 was on 5 February 2000, so the Year of the Dragon had not started yet.

Someone born on 28 January 1990 is a Horse: Lunar New Year 1990 fell on 27 January 1990, one day earlier. A baby born on 25 January 1990 is still a Snake.

What about Spring Commences (Lichun)?

Some astrologers count the zodiac year from the solar term Lichun, Spring Commences, instead of from Lunar New Year. Wikipedia's article on the sexagenary cycle notes this difference. Lichun falls around 4 February; the Hong Kong Observatory places it on 4 February in 2027.

For January birthdays this changes nothing: January is always before Lichun, so by that method too, everyone born in January belongs to the previous year's animal. The two methods only disagree for some birthdays in late January and early February. Our site follows Lunar New Year, the common everyday method.

And the Khmer animal year?

In Cambodia there is a third answer. The Khmer animal year changes at Khmer New Year in mid-April, so by the Khmer count everyone born between January and mid-April also belongs to the previous animal. Our guide to Khmer New Year and the animal year explains why.

Frequently asked questions

What is my Chinese zodiac if I was born in January?

Most likely the previous year's animal. The zodiac year starts at Lunar New Year, between 21 January and 20 February, so anyone born 1–20 January belongs to the previous year's animal, and late-January birthdays depend on the year.

I was born on 25 January 1990. Am I a Horse or a Snake?

A Snake. Lunar New Year 1990 fell on 27 January 1990, so the Year of the Horse started two days after your birthday.

When does the Chinese zodiac year start?

On Lunar New Year, which falls between 21 January and 20 February. In 2027 it is 6 February, when the Year of the Goat begins.

Is anyone born in January 2027 a Goat?

No. Lunar New Year 2027 is on 6 February, so babies born in January 2027 are Horses.
