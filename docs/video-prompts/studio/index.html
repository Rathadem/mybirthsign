MyBirthSign Video Studio

Pick a date and get that day's full video pack: picture prompts, Google Flow scenes with voice, captions, voice-overs, YouTube, Facebook and TikTok text, and subtitles. Every rating and lucky number follows the website's own rules for that date.

  1. Once: make the 12 character pictures (Character prompts below) and keep them.
  2. Each day: in Google Flow, add the characters as ingredients, then paste each scene with “Copy + English voice” or “Copy + Khmer voice”.
  3. Put the clips in order 1, 2, 3, 5, 4, add the on-screen captions, then post with the YouTube, Facebook and TikTok text.
