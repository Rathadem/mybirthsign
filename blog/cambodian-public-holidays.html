Cambodian Public Holidays 2026

✦ every official holiday, what it means, and which dates move each year ✦

In 2026 Cambodia has 15 public holidays covering 22 days off, set by Sub-Decree No. 167. Some keep the same date every year, some follow the moon and one follows the sun. Here is the full list, what each one marks, and the expected 2027 dates of the moving ones.

Three kinds of holiday date

  • Fixed date

    Same Gregorian date every year, such as Independence Day on 9 November.

  • Lunar date

    Visak Bochea, the Royal Ploughing Ceremony, Pchum Ben and the Water Festival follow the Khmer lunar calendar, so they move by up to about a month from year to year.

  • Solar date

    Khmer New Year follows the sun's move into Aries, so it stays on 13–16 April.

What each holiday marks

History and nation

  • Victory over Genocide Day — Marks 7 January 1979, when the Khmer Rouge regime was driven from Phnom Penh.
  • Constitution Day — Marks the 1993 Constitution, which restored the constitutional monarchy.
  • Independence Day — Independence from France on 9 November 1953.
  • Peace Day — Marks the end of the civil war on 29 December 1998; a public holiday since 2024.

Royal days

  • Royal Ploughing Ceremony — A royal rite at the start of the rice season, held four days after Visak Bochea.
  • King Norodom Sihamoni's Birthday — The birthday of the reigning King, born 14 May 1953.
  • Queen Mother Norodom Monineath Sihanouk's Birthday — The birthday of the Queen Mother, the King's mother.
  • King Father Norodom Sihanouk Commemoration Day — King Father Norodom Sihanouk died on 15 October 2012.
  • Coronation Day of King Norodom Sihamoni — King Norodom Sihamoni was crowned on 29 October 2004.

Religious and traditional

  • Khmer New Year — The traditional New Year, beginning at Moha Songkran in mid-April.
  • Visak Bochea — Remembers the Buddha's birth, enlightenment and passing, on the full moon of Pisak.
  • Pchum Ben — Cambodia's festival for the dead, at the end of the month of Phatrabot.
  • Water Festival (Bon Om Touk) — Boat races, illuminated floats and the salute to the moon, at the full moon of Kadeuk.

International days

  • International New Year's Day — The start of the Gregorian year.
  • International Women's Day — Honours women's rights and contributions.
  • International Labour Day — Honours workers; in 2026 it falls on the same day as Visak Bochea.

Planning around the holidays

  • Government offices, schools and banks close on public holidays.
  • Pchum Ben and Khmer New Year are the big travel times: many people go home to their province, buses fill up and Phnom Penh becomes quiet.
  • During the Water Festival, boat races on the river draw large crowds to the Phnom Penh riverside.
  • Lunar holidays can move by weeks, so check the year before booking. Our Khmer Calendar shows them for any month.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many public holidays does Cambodia have in 2026?

Fifteen holidays covering 22 days, because Khmer New Year, Pchum Ben and the Water Festival last three days each, and Visak Bochea shares 1 May with Labour Day.

Which Cambodian holidays change date every year?

Visak Bochea, the Royal Ploughing Ceremony, Pchum Ben and the Water Festival, because they follow the lunar calendar. Khmer New Year stays in mid-April.

Is Meak Bochea a public holiday in Cambodia?

Not in 2026: it is not on the official list, although it remains an important Buddhist day. See our guide to Cambodian Buddhist holy days.

Is Chinese New Year a public holiday in Cambodia?

It is not on the official 2026 list.

When will the 2027 holidays be confirmed?

When the government publishes the 2027 sub-decree. Until then, the 2027 dates on this page are calculated from the lunar calendar rules.

What is Peace Day?

Peace Day on 29 December marks the end of Cambodia's civil war on 29 December 1998. It became a national holiday by a sub-decree in 2024.

ប្រវត្តិសាស្ត្រ និងជាតិ

  • ទិវាជ័យជម្នះលើរបបប្រល័យពូជសាសន៍ — រំលឹកថ្ងៃ 7 មករា 1979 ដែលរបបខ្មែរក្រហមត្រូវបានដេញចេញពីភ្នំពេញ។
  • ទិវាប្រកាសរដ្ឋធម្មនុញ្ញ — រំលឹកដល់រដ្ឋធម្មនុញ្ញឆ្នាំ 1993 ដែលស្តាររាជាធិបតេយ្យអាស្រ័យរដ្ឋធម្មនុញ្ញឡើងវិញ។
  • ពិធីបុណ្យឯករាជ្យជាតិ — ឯករាជ្យពីប្រទេសបារាំង នៅថ្ងៃ 9 វិច្ឆិកា 1953។
  • ទិវាសន្តិភាព — រំលឹកការបញ្ចប់សង្គ្រាមស៊ីវិល នៅថ្ងៃ 29 ធ្នូ 1998 ហើយជាថ្ងៃឈប់សម្រាកតាំងពីឆ្នាំ 2024។

សាសនា និងប្រពៃណី

  • ពិធីបុណ្យចូលឆ្នាំថ្មី ប្រពៃណីជាតិ — ឆ្នាំថ្មីប្រពៃណី ដែលចាប់ផ្តើមនៅមហាសង្ក្រាន្ត ពាក់កណ្តាលខែមេសា។

三种假期日期

  • 固定日期

    每年公历日期相同，例如11月9日的独立日。

  • 农历日期

    卫塞节、御耕节、亡人节和送水节按高棉农历计算，每年可相差约一个月。

  • 阳历日期

    高棉新年跟随太阳进入白羊宫的时间，所以固定在4月13日至16日之间。

每个假期纪念什么

历史与国家

  • 战胜种族灭绝纪念日 — 纪念1979年1月7日红色高棉政权被逐出金边。
  • 宪法日 — 纪念1993年宪法，该宪法恢复了君主立宪制。
  • 独立日 — 1953年11月9日脱离法国独立。
  • 和平日 — 纪念1998年12月29日内战结束；自2024年起成为公共假期。

王室纪念日

  • 御耕节 — 在稻作季节开始时举行的王室仪式，于卫塞节后四天举行。
  • 西哈莫尼国王诞辰 — 在位国王的生日，他生于1953年5月14日。
  • 莫尼列太后诞辰 — 太后（国王的母亲）的生日。
  • 西哈努克太皇纪念日 — 西哈努克太皇于2012年10月15日逝世。
  • 西哈莫尼国王登基纪念日 — 西哈莫尼国王于2004年10月29日登基。

宗教与传统

  • 高棉新年 — 传统新年，从四月中旬的 Moha Songkran 开始。
  • 卫塞节 — 在 Pisak 月满月纪念佛陀的诞生、成道和涅槃。
  • 亡人节 — 柬埔寨的祭祖节，在 Phatrabot 月末。
  • 送水节 — 在 Kadeuk 月满月举行龙舟赛、灯船和拜月。

国际节日

  • 元旦 — 公历新年的开始。
  • 国际妇女节 — 表彰女性的权利与贡献。
  • 国际劳动节 — 向劳动者致敬；2026年与卫塞节同一天。

假期出行与安排

  • 公共假期期间政府机关、学校和银行休息。
  • 亡人节和高棉新年是出行高峰：许多人回乡，巴士爆满，金边变得冷清。
  • 送水节期间，河上的龙舟赛吸引大批人群前往金边河滨。
  • 农历假期每年可能相差几周，订票前请先查看。我们的高棉日历可显示任意月份的假期。

常见问题

柬埔寨2026年有多少个公共假期？

15个假期，合计22天，因为高棉新年、亡人节和送水节各放三天，而卫塞节与劳动节同在5月1日。

哪些柬埔寨假期每年日期不同？

卫塞节、御耕节、亡人节和送水节，因为它们按农历计算。高棉新年固定在四月中旬。

万佛节是柬埔寨的公共假期吗？

2026年不是：它不在官方名单中，但仍是重要的佛教节日。

春节是柬埔寨的公共假期吗？

它不在2026年的官方名单中。

2027年的假期何时确认？

在政府公布2027年次级法令时。在此之前，本页的2027年日期是按农历规则计算的。

和平日是什么？

12月29日的和平日纪念1998年12月29日柬埔寨内战结束。它于2024年通过次级法令成为国家假日。
