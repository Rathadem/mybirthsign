Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MyBirthSign ("we", "our", "the site") handles information when you visit mybirthsign.com.

Information We Collect

We do not create user accounts or keep visitor profiles. The zodiac checker, compatibility and other calculators run entirely in your browser — the birth dates you enter are not sent to or stored on our servers (on the compatibility page they may appear in your own browser's address bar so you can reload the result; the share buttons never include them). We do not currently use a separate analytics tool. Like every website, our hosting provider, Netlify, processes technical request data (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) to deliver and protect the site.

Cookies and Advertising

This site may use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners may use cookies, device identifiers, or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses data and manage your ad personalization settings at Google's Ads Policy page.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on those visits. You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google's Ads Settings. Where the law requires it, you may be asked for your consent before personalized ads are shown.

We may also show ads from other advertising partners, such as Adsterra and Media.net. Like Google, these partners and the advertisers they work with may use cookies, device identifiers or similar technologies to measure ads and to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising from many companies at the Network Advertising Initiative and the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out pages, and you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. MyBirthSign does not sell your personal information, and birth dates you enter in our tools are not sent to advertisers.

Fonts and Images from Other Services

Pages load their fonts from Google Fonts, and the Khmer Calendar page loads its background picture from Unsplash. When your browser requests these files, those services receive technical data such as your IP address and browser type.

Daily Fortune, Sharing and Language

The daily Chinese zodiac readings are prepared once a day by our own scheduled job (traditional rules plus wording written with Anthropic's Claude AI) and published as a public file; they contain no visitor information. When you use a Share button, the link contains only the zodiac animals, the date and your language — never names or birth dates. Shared and downloaded pictures are drawn on your own device and are not uploaded to or stored by us. The social network or app you choose (such as Facebook, WhatsApp, Telegram, Pinterest, Instagram or TikTok) receives what you share and handles it under its own privacy policy, and services like Facebook and Telegram may visit the shared link to show a preview. Your language choice (English, Khmer or Chinese) is remembered in your own browser only.

Third-Party Links

This site may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those external sites.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via our contact page.
