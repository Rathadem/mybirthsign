Rat and Dragon Compatibility: Love, Friendship and Business

Rat and Dragon are one of the classic good matches of the Chinese zodiac. Here is why tradition pairs them, how the two personalities fit in love, friendship and work, and where they need to take care.

Published October 27, 2026 · By Ratha, MyBirthSign

RelationshipSame triangle
Love5 of 5 stars
Business5 of 5 stars

The short answer: a natural match

In traditional Chinese compatibility, the twelve animals form four triangles of signs that are said to share outlook and energy. The Rat and the Dragon sit in the same one: Rat, Dragon and Monkey. Pairs within a triangle are traditionally counted among the easiest matches, which is why our compatibility calculator gives Rat and Dragon its highest quick rating: 5 of 5 stars for love and 5 of 5 for business.

The Rat also lists the Dragon among its best matches in our Rat guide, and the Dragon lists the Rat in the Dragon guide. They are never in a clash: the Rat's opposite sign is the Horse, and the Dragon's is the Dog.

Why the two personalities fit

Our animal guides describe the Rat as “Quick-witted, resourceful, and sociable. Rats are natural problem-solvers who notice opportunities others miss.” and the Dragon as “Confident, charismatic, and ambitious. Dragons draw attention and often rise to leadership naturally.”

Put simply, the Dragon brings the big vision and the confidence to lead, and the Rat brings sharp observation and practical problem-solving. The Dragon is good at opening doors; the Rat is good at noticing which door is worth opening. Each tends to admire what the other does best, which is the traditional reason the pairing works.

Rat and Dragon in love

As a couple, Rat and Dragon are traditionally said to understand each other's pace and ambitions without much explaining. Both like a full, active life, and both are sociable, so a shared circle of friends tends to grow around them.

The Rat is usually the one who keeps an eye on details and money; the Dragon is usually the one who wants to aim higher. That works well when each respects the other's role, and less well when the Dragon's bold plans meet the Rat's caution without a conversation.

Rat and Dragon as friends

As friends, the pair tends to be lively and loyal. The Rat enjoys the Dragon's energy and big ideas; the Dragon values a friend who is quick, honest and well-informed. They are often the friends who start projects together, from a trip to a small business.

Rat and Dragon in business

In work, the traditional reading is especially strong. The Dragon is a natural public face and leader; the Rat is a natural researcher, negotiator and keeper of the numbers. Our animal guides suggest careers such as business, research and trading for the Rat, and leadership, entrepreneurship and real estate for the Dragon, which divide neatly into the roles of a partnership.

For a deeper look at work partnerships, try our business partner match, which compares two birth dates.

What to watch out for

Even a good match has friction points. The ones tradition and our animal guides point to are:

  • Pace and risk. The Rat can be restless or overly cautious with money and trust. The Dragon can come across as proud or impatient with limitations. Talk through big decisions before either side commits.
  • Credit. The Dragon likes to be seen leading; the Rat often does the quiet work behind a success. Say thank you out loud, both ways.
  • Pride. Two confident signs can both want the last word. Agree in advance who decides what.

How birth elements change the match

The animal is only part of a traditional reading. Each birth year also has an element, and the element of the year changes the flavour of the match. These are the recent Rat and Dragon years (each starts at Lunar New Year, so check January and early-February birthdays):

  • Rat years: 1948 (Earth, from 10 February 1948); 1960 (Metal, from 28 January 1960); 1972 (Water, from 15 February 1972); 1984 (Wood, from 2 February 1984); 1996 (Fire, from 19 February 1996); 2008 (Earth, from 7 February 2008); 2020 (Metal, from 25 January 2020)
  • Dragon years: 1952 (Water, from 27 January 1952); 1964 (Wood, from 13 February 1964); 1976 (Fire, from 31 January 1976); 1988 (Earth, from 17 February 1988); 2000 (Metal, from 5 February 2000); 2012 (Water, from 23 January 2012); 2024 (Wood, from 10 February 2024)

In the generating cycle of the five elements, Wood feeds Fire, Fire makes Earth, Earth bears Metal, Metal enriches Water and Water nourishes Wood. Pairs whose elements follow each other in that cycle, such as a Water Rat with a Wood Dragon, are traditionally read as especially supportive. Frequently asked questions

Are Rat and Dragon compatible?

Yes. They share the Rat–Dragon–Monkey triangle, which tradition counts among the easiest matches. Our calculator gives the pair 5 of 5 stars for love and for business.

Is a Rat man and Dragon woman a good match?

The traditional reading is the same either way round: the animals, not the gender, decide the triangle. Exact scores in our calculator use both full birth dates.

What is the Rat's best match?

Our Rat guide lists the Dragon, the Monkey and the Ox as the Rat's best matches. The Horse is the Rat's clash sign.

Do Rat and Dragon clash?

No. The Rat clashes with the Horse and the Dragon with the Dog. Rat and Dragon sit in the same triangle.
