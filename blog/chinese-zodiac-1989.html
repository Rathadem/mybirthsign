1989 Chinese Zodiac: Year of the Earth Snake

Born in 1989? Your Chinese zodiac animal is most likely the Snake, but check the date: the 1989 Earth Snake year began on 6 February 1989. Here is everything about the year, from its element to its lucky colors and best matches.

1989: a Yin Earth Snake year

The Chinese zodiac year 1989 was the Year of the Snake. It began with Lunar New Year on 6 February 1989 and ended on 26 January 1990, the eve of the next Lunar New Year.

Each year also has an element and a Yin or Yang quality. 1989 ends in 9, which makes it an Earth year, and as an odd year it is Yin. In the traditional stem-and-branch count, 1989 is a Ji-si year. The same Earth Snake combination comes round only once every 60 years: 1929 before it and 2049 after it.

Born in January or February 1989?

Lunar New Year 1989 fell on 6 February 1989, so everyone born in January 1989, and in February before that date, belongs to the previous year's animal, the Dragon.

The zodiac year starts at Lunar New Year, not on 1 January, which is why early-year birthdays need checking. Our guide Born in January? explains the rule, and the zodiac sign checker gives the exact answer for any birth date.

The Snake's personality

Our Snake guide describes the sign like this: “Wise, intuitive, and elegant. Snakes are deep thinkers who plan carefully before acting.” The usual watch-out: “Can be secretive or prone to overthinking.”

The Earth element adds its own flavour. Earth years bring stability, practicality, and patience. People of this element tend to be dependable, grounded, and good at long-term planning. Read more in our Earth element guide, or the full Year of the Snake page.

Careers often linked with the Snake: finance, research, psychology, strategy.

Lucky colors, numbers and days for the Snake

  • Lucky colors: black, red and yellow
  • Lucky numbers: 2, 8 and 9
  • Lucky days: Wednesday and Saturday

Best matches for someone born in 1989

In traditional compatibility, the Snake's best matches are the Ox, Rooster and Monkey, and its clash sign is the Pig, which sits directly opposite it on the zodiac circle. Our compatibility calculator compares two full birth dates, elements included.

2026 (Fire Horse, until 5 February 2027): for the Snake, it has no special traditional relationship with the Horse, so 2026 is a neutral year.
