    How to read the Khmer lunar calendar

    Cambodia uses two calendars side by side. Everyday life runs on the Gregorian calendar, while Buddhist holy days, Pchum Ben, the Water Festival, Kathen and many family occasions follow the Khmer lunar calendar. This page shows both at once: tap any day to see its Khmer lunar date, its animal year, its Sak and its Buddhist Era year.

    Waxing and waning days (Kaet and Roach)

    Khmer lunar dates are not numbered 1 to 30. Each month follows one cycle of the moon, about 29½ days, so months alternate between 29 and 30 days, and each month has two halves. In the waxing half the days run from 1 Kaet to 15 Kaet, and 15 Kaet is the full-moon day. In the waning half they run from 1 Roach to 14 Roach in a 29-day month, or to 15 Roach in a 30-day month; the last day is the dark-moon day. So "8 Roach, month of Phatrabot" means the eighth day of the waning half of Phatrabot.

    The twelve months

    In their traditional order: Migasir, Boss, Meak, Phalkun, Chet, Pisak, Jesth, Asadh, Srap, Phatrabot, Assoch and Kadeuk. Romanized spellings vary, so you may see the same month written in slightly different ways.

    Leap days and leap months

    Twelve moons make only about 354 days, eleven days short of a solar year. To keep the festivals in their seasons, some years get one extra day (Chantreathimeas, 355 days, when Jesth has 30 days instead of 29) and some get a whole extra month (Athikameas, 384 days, when Asadh is repeated as Pathamasadh and Tutiyasadh). 2026 is a leap-month year, which is why Pchum Ben came late this year. Our guide to Khmer leap months explains how the rules work.

    Holy days in every month

    Four days in each lunar month are Thngai Sil, Buddhist precept days: 8 Kaet, the full moon on 15 Kaet, 8 Roach and the last waning day. The 🪷 mark on the calendar shows Buddhist observances; see Cambodian Buddhist holy days for what each one means.

    The year name: animal, Sak and Buddhist Era

    A full Khmer date often ends with a year name such as "Year of the Horse, Atthasak, B.E. 2570". Each part changes on a different day: the animal changes at Moha Songkran, the start of Khmer New Year in mid-April; the Sak moves up by one on Loeng Sak, the last day of Khmer New Year; and the Buddhist Era year changes the day after Visak Bochea. That is why the Khmer animal year starts in April, not at Chinese New Year. Read more in Khmer animal year and Sak and when the animal year really changes.

    How the dates on this page are worked out

    Lunar dates come from the traditional Khmer calendar rules, using the open-source momentkh library that our Zodiac Checker also uses; they are calculated, not estimated by hand. The calendar's full-moon day can differ by a day from an astronomy app, because the Khmer calendar follows its own rules rather than nightly observation. Public holidays are announced each year by the Royal Government of Cambodia, so check the official announcement before you plan travel.     如何看高棉农历

    柬埔寨同时使用两种历法。日常生活用公历，而佛教持戒日、亡人节（Pchum Ben）、送水节、迦提那节和许多家庭活动都按高棉农历进行。本页同时显示两种日期：点击任何一天，即可看到它的高棉农历日期、生肖年、Sak 和佛历年份。

    上半月与下半月（Kaet 与 Roach）

    高棉农历的日期不是从 1 数到 30。每个月对应月亮的一个周期，约 29.5 天，所以大月 30 天、小月 29 天交替出现，每个月分为两半。上半月（月盈）从 1 Kaet 数到 15 Kaet，15 Kaet 是满月日；下半月（月亏）从 1 Roach 数到 14 Roach（29 天的月）或 15 Roach（30 天的月），最后一天是无月日。

    十二个月

    按传统顺序：Migasir、Boss、Meak、Phalkun、Chet、Pisak、Jesth、Asadh、Srap、Phatrabot、Assoch、Kadeuk。拼写方式可能略有不同。

    闰日与闰月

    十二个月只有约 354 天，比太阳年短约 11 天。为了让节日保持在原来的季节，有些年份加一天（355 天，Jesth 月为 30 天），有些年份加一整个月（384 天，Asadh 月重复两次）。2026 年是闰月年，所以今年的亡人节比较晚。

    每月的持戒日

    每个农历月有四个持戒日（Thngai Sil）：8 Kaet、满月 15 Kaet、8 Roach 和下半月的最后一天。日历上的 🪷 标记表示佛教节日。

    年份名称：生肖、Sak 与佛历

    完整的高棉日期常以年份名称结尾，例如“马年，Atthasak，佛历 2570 年”。各部分在不同的日子更换：生肖在四月中旬高棉新年开始时（Moha Songkran）更换；Sak 在高棉新年最后一天（Loeng Sak）加一；佛历在卫塞节（Visak Bochea）的第二天更换。所以高棉生肖年从四月开始，而不是从中国春节开始。

    本页日期如何计算

    农历日期按传统高棉历法规则计算，使用开源的 momentkh 程序库（我们的生肖查询工具也使用它）。日历上的满月日可能与天文应用相差一天，因为高棉历法遵循自己的规则。公共假日每年由柬埔寨王国政府公布，出行前请查看官方公告。想查自己的农历生日，请使用生肖查询。
