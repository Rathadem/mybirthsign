Contact Us

Have a question, correction, or feedback about MyBirthSign? We'd love to hear from you.

Email: contact@mybirthsign.com

What to write to us about

  • A result or date that looks wrong. Tell us the page address and the date you tried, and what you expected to see.
  • A translation in Khmer or Chinese that reads awkwardly or is incorrect.
  • Something that doesn't work, such as a broken link, a button or a layout problem. Your phone or browser name helps.
  • An idea for a guide or a tradition you would like explained.

Messages you send are handled as described in our Privacy Policy. To learn how the calculators work first, see About MyBirthSign.
