The Spirit of the Dragon

The Dragon traditionally represents confidence, charisma, and the ambition to rise.

Understanding the Dragon

The Dragon is the only mythical creature in the zodiac, and it carries that larger-than-life energy. Dragons tend to walk into a room and shift its center of gravity without even trying — people notice them, trust them, and often hand them the lead role before they've asked for it. That natural authority comes paired with high standards, both for themselves and everyone around them, which can read as impatience when reality moves slower than their vision does.

Personality

Confident, charismatic, and ambitious. Dragons draw attention and often rise to leadership naturally.

People tend to defer to a Dragon almost on instinct, which gives them real influence from an early age — but it also means Dragons rarely get told "no" as often as everyone else does, so they have to work a little harder to notice when they've actually overstepped.

In relationships

Dragons love with the same intensity they bring to everything else — generous, protective, and genuinely proud to be seen with the people they care about. They do best with partners confident enough not to be overshadowed by that energy, and who'll tell them the truth even when it's not what the Dragon wants to hear.

At work

Dragons gravitate naturally toward leadership, and people tend to follow them without much convincing. They do their best work when given a big vision to chase, and their least best work in roles that ask them to quietly follow someone else's smaller plan.

What to watch for

Can come across as proud or impatient with limitations. That confidence is an asset right up until it stops someone from hearing feedback, so a Dragon benefits from actively asking for honest pushback rather than waiting for it to show up uninvited.

Compatibility

Dragons shine brightest alongside ambitious signs who can match their energy without competing for the spotlight, and run into the most friction with signs who feel boxed in by a Dragon's big plans.

Best matches: Rat, Monkey, Rooster

Can clash with: Dog

Careers that often fit well

Roles that come with real authority and a big stage to perform on tend to bring out the best in a Dragon.

Frequently Asked Questions

What years are the Year of the Dragon?

Dragon years include 1964, 1976, 1988, 2000, 2012, 2024, 2036. The zodiac repeats every 12 years, and the zodiac year begins at Lunar New Year, not on January 1.

What personality traits are associated with the Dragon?

Traditionally, the Dragon is associated with courage, confidence, independence, and determination. Confident, charismatic, and ambitious. Dragons draw attention and often rise to leadership naturally.

What are the lucky colors for the Dragon?

The Dragon's lucky colors in our guide are Gold, Silver, Gray.

What are the lucky numbers for the Dragon?

The Dragon's lucky numbers are 1, 6, 7.

Which zodiac animals are compatible with the Dragon?

Best matches are Rat and Monkey, which share the Dragon's compatibility triangle. Rooster is also listed as a supportive match in our guide, while Dog can be the most challenging pairing.

What element is my Dragon year?

It depends on your birth year — Wood: 1964, 2024 · Fire: 1976, 2036 · Earth: 1988 · Metal: 2000 · Water: 2012. Use the Zodiac Checker to confirm yours.

Does the Chinese zodiac year start on January 1?

No. The zodiac year begins at Lunar New Year, which falls between late January and mid-February. If you were born in January or February, check the Lunar New Year date or use our Zodiac Checker.

Is Chinese zodiac personality scientifically proven?

No. Chinese zodiac interpretations are traditional and cultural, and they are not scientifically proven. Enjoy them for fun and self-reflection.

សំណួរដែលសួរញឹកញាប់

តើឆ្នាំរោងគឺឆ្នាំអ្វីខ្លះ?

ឆ្នាំរោងរួមមាន 1964, 1976, 1988, 2000, 2012, 2024, 2036។ រង្វង់រាសីដដែលៗរៀងរាល់ ១២ឆ្នាំម្តង ហើយឆ្នាំរាសីចាប់ផ្តើមនៅថ្ងៃចូលឆ្នាំចន្ទគតិ មិនមែនថ្ងៃទី១ មករាទេ។

តើបុគ្គលិកលក្ខណៈអ្វីខ្លះដែលផ្សារភ្ជាប់នឹងរោង?

តាមប្រពៃណី រោងត្រូវបានផ្សារភ្ជាប់ជាមួយភាពក្លាហាន ទំនុកចិត្ត ឯករាជ្យភាព និងការតាំងចិត្ត។ ជឿជាក់លើខ្លួនឯង ទាក់ទាញចំណាប់អារម្មណ៍ និងមានមហិច្ឆតា។ រោងជាអ្នកដឹកនាំដោយធម្មជាតិ ហើយតែងទទួលបានការគោរពពីអ្នកដទៃ។

តើពណ៌សំណាងរបស់រោងជាអ្វី?

ពណ៌សំណាងរបស់រោងក្នុងមគ្គុទ្ទេសក៍របស់យើងគឺ មាស, ប្រាក់, ប្រផេះ។

តើលេខសំណាងរបស់រោងជាអ្វី?

លេខសំណាងរបស់រោងគឺ 1, 6, 7។

តើសត្វណាខ្លះត្រូវគ្នាជាមួយរោង?

ត្រូវគ្នាបំផុតជាមួយ ជូត, វក (ក្រុមតែមួយ)។ រកា ក៏ត្រូវបានរាប់ជាដៃគូជួយគាំទ្រក្នុងមគ្គុទ្ទេសក៍របស់យើង។ ច អាចមានភាពប្រឈមជាងគេ។

តើរោងរបស់ខ្ញុំជាធាតុអ្វី?

ធាតុអាស្រ័យលើឆ្នាំកំណើតរបស់អ្នក — ឈើ៖ 1964, 2024 · ភ្លើង៖ 1976, 2036 · ដី៖ 1988 · លោហធាតុ៖ 2000 · ទឹក៖ 2012។ ប្រើម៉ាស៊ីនពិនិត្យរាសីដើម្បីបញ្ជាក់ឆ្នាំរបស់អ្នក។

តើឆ្នាំរាសីចាប់ផ្តើមថ្ងៃទី១ មករាឬទេ?

ទេ។ ឆ្នាំរាសីចាប់ផ្តើមនៅថ្ងៃចូលឆ្នាំចន្ទគតិ ដែលធ្លាក់នៅចន្លោះចុងខែមករា និងពាក់កណ្តាលខែកុម្ភៈ។ ប្រសិនបើអ្នកកើតក្នុងខែទាំងពីរនេះ សូមពិនិត្យជាមួយម៉ាស៊ីនពិនិត្យរបស់យើង។

តើបុគ្គលិកលក្ខណៈតាមរាសីត្រូវបានបញ្ជាក់ដោយវិទ្យាសាស្ត្រឬទេ?
