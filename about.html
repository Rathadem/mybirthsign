About MyBirthSign

MyBirthSign is a small, independent website built for anyone curious about what their birthday might reveal through the lens of the traditional Chinese zodiac. Enter your date of birth and instantly see your zodiac animal, element, lucky numbers and colors, compatibility matches, and general career fit.

We built this site because, while zodiac information is everywhere online, it's often scattered, inconsistent, or buried under ads before you get an answer. Our goal is a clean, fast tool paired with clear, honestly-written articles about how the system actually works — including its limitations, like the lunar new year cutoff explained in our blog.

Everything on this site is intended for entertainment and self-reflection purposes. We don't claim any scientific basis for zodiac predictions, and we encourage visitors to treat results as a fun starting point rather than a serious life guide.

What you can do here

  • Zodiac Checker: your animal, element and Yin or Yang from your birth date, plus your Khmer lunar birth date.
  • Compatibility: compare two birth dates, or two signs if you don't know the dates.
  • Business Partner and Wedding Date: the same traditions applied to a partnership and to choosing a wedding month.
  • Guides to all 12 animals, plus articles on calculating your sign, the five elements and the Khmer lunar calendar.

How the results are worked out

  • Lunar New Year cutoff. Every calculator compares your birth date with the actual Lunar New Year date for that year, from a table covering 1900 to 2060. We checked every date in that table against an independent lunar-calendar library and found no differences.
  • Element and Yin or Yang come from the zodiac year's last digit, following the traditional ten-stem cycle (for example, years ending in 0 or 1 are Metal).
  • Compatibility uses the traditional triangles, six harmony pairs and clashes. The compatibility guide explains each rule, and each tool says which rules it uses.
  • Khmer lunar dates are calculated with the open-source momentkh library, never estimated by hand.
  • Your dates stay with you. Calculations run in your browser, and birth dates and names are never put into share links.

How we write and correct content

Articles describe traditional beliefs as traditions, explain where they come from, and never promise outcomes. Where traditions disagree, for example on when the animal year changes in the Chinese and Khmer calendars, we explain both instead of quietly choosing one. If you find a wrong date, result or translation, please tell us and we will correct the page.

Daily fortune ratings, lucky numbers and calendar dates come from fixed rules and data in the site's code. The wording of each day's fortune is drafted with an AI model (Anthropic's Claude) and is published only after it passes automatic checks against those rules. Other articles may also be drafted with AI help, but their dates and facts come from the calculators or from the sources listed on the page.

Languages

Pages are available in English, Khmer and Simplified Chinese, each at its own address; a few articles, such as the business compatibility guide, are not yet translated into every language. Use the language buttons at the top of any page. Khmer pages carry no advertising.

Have a question, correction, or suggestion? Visit our contact page.

关于 MyBirthSign

MyBirthSign 是一个小型独立网站，为所有好奇自己的生日在传统中国生肖中意味着什么的人而建。输入你的出生日期，即可马上看到你的生肖、五行、幸运数字和颜色、配对以及大致适合的职业。

我们建立这个网站，是因为网上的生肖资料虽然到处都有，却常常零散、前后不一，或者在你找到答案之前就被广告淹没。我们的目标是提供一个简洁、快速的工具，再配上清楚、诚实的文章，讲解这个体系实际上如何运作——包括它的局限，比如我们博客中解释的农历新年分界。

本网站的所有内容仅供娱乐与自我反思。我们不声称生肖预测有任何科学依据，也建议访客把结果当作有趣的起点，而不是严肃的人生指南。

你可以在这里做什么

  • 生肖查询：根据出生日期查出你的生肖、五行和阴阳，以及你的高棉农历生日。
  • 配对：比较两个生日；如果不知道生日，也可以直接比较两个生肖。
  • 商业伙伴和婚期：把同样的传统用在合作关系和挑选结婚月份上。
  • 十二生肖指南，以及关于如何计算生肖、五行和高棉农历的文章。

结果是怎样算出来的

  • 农历新年分界。每个计算器都会把你的出生日期与当年真实的农历新年日期比较，日期表涵盖1900年至2060年。我们用一个独立的农历程序库逐一核对了表中的每个日期，没有发现任何差异。
  • 五行与阴阳由生肖年份的最后一位数字决定，遵循传统的十天干循环（例如尾数为0或1的年份属金）。
  • 配对使用传统的三合、六合与六冲。配对指南解释了每条规则，每个工具也会说明它使用哪些规则。
  • 高棉农历日期由开源程序库 momentkh 计算，从不人工估算。
  • 你的日期只留在你这里。计算在你的浏览器中进行，生日和姓名从不会放进分享链接。

我们如何撰写和更正内容

文章把传统信仰作为传统来介绍，说明它们的来历，从不承诺结果。当不同传统说法不一，例如中国和高棉历法中生肖年何时更换，我们会把两种都解释清楚，而不是悄悄只选一种。如果你发现日期、结果或翻译有误，请告诉我们，我们会更正页面。

每日运势的评分、幸运数字和历法日期都来自网站代码中固定的规则和数据。每天运势的文字由 AI 模型（Anthropic 的 Claude）起草，只有通过与这些规则的自动核对后才会发布。其他文章也可能借助 AI 起草，但其中的日期和事实来自计算工具或页面所列的来源。

语言

页面提供英文、高棉文和简体中文版本，各有自己的网址；少数文章（例如商业配对指南）尚未翻译成所有语言。可以使用任意页面顶部的语言按钮切换。高棉文页面不展示广告。
