Its hour of the day

The Goat rules the early afternoon, from 1 to 3 p.m. A traditional explanation is that this is a good time for goats to graze, after the heat of noon has passed. Its animal is translated as both Goat and Sheep, which is why English sources use both names.

Its place in the Great Race

In the Great Race folk tale the Goat did not cross alone but shared a raft with the Monkey and the Rooster, and the three helped each other reach the far bank, arriving eighth, ninth and tenth. The story suits the Goat's gentle, cooperative reputation.

Earth is the Goat's own element and stays the same; the element in the dates table changes with the birth year, and the two match in 1979 and 2039.

The Spirit of the Goat

The Goat traditionally represents gentleness, creativity, and a caring heart.

Understanding the Goat

The Goat (sometimes translated as Sheep) is the zodiac's gentle artist, drawn to beauty, harmony, and emotional depth. Goats tend to notice feelings that others miss, making them warm friends and genuinely talented in creative fields. They do their best work in a secure, supportive environment, and tend to struggle more in cutthroat or highly competitive settings. Left without that support, a Goat can become indecisive or lean too heavily on others to make the hard calls for them.

Personality

Gentle, creative, and compassionate. Goats have a strong artistic sensibility and care deeply for others.

Goats notice beauty and emotional undercurrents that most people walk right past, which makes them unusually good company and genuinely gifted at creative or caretaking work. They flourish fastest around people who are patient with their sensitivity rather than impatient for them to toughen up.

In relationships

Goats are warm, attentive partners who put real thought into making the people they love feel cared for. They do best with a partner who can take the lead on hard practical decisions sometimes, since a Goat's instinct under pressure is to look to someone else rather than decide alone.

At work

Goats bring real craft and emotional intelligence to creative or people-focused work, and tend to do their best in supportive, low-conflict teams. Cutthroat, highly competitive workplaces usually bring out their worst, not their best.

What to watch for

Can be indecisive or overly dependent on others. Left to make a hard call alone, a Goat can freeze up or quietly hand the decision to someone else — a little practice making small decisions solo tends to help.

Compatibility

Goats bond most easily with warm, patient signs who create the secure environment they need to flourish, and struggle more with blunt or highly competitive signs.

Can clash with: Ox

Careers that often fit well

Frequently Asked Questions

What years are the Year of the Goat?

Goat years include 1967, 1979, 1991, 2003, 2015, 2027, 2039. Each one begins at Lunar New Year, not on 1 January: the 1991 Goat year ran from 15 February 1991 to 3 February 1992.

What personality traits are associated with the Goat?

People born in the Year of the Goat are traditionally associated with gentleness, creativity, and compassion. The strengths most often listed are gentle and compassionate, creative and artistic, cares deeply for others, calm and kind, strong sense of beauty. The usual watch-outs are can be indecisive, may depend on others too much, can be sensitive to stress, may avoid decisions.

What are the Goat's lucky colors and numbers?

In our guide the Goat's lucky colors are Green, Red, Purple and its lucky numbers are 2, 7. Sources differ on these lists.

Which zodiac animals are compatible with the Goat?

Best matches are Rabbit and Pig, which share the Goat's compatibility triangle. Horse is also listed as a supportive match in our guide, while Ox can be the most challenging pairing.

What element is my Goat year?

It depends on your birth year — Wood: 2015 · Fire: 1967, 2027 · Earth: 1979, 2039 · Metal: 1991 · Water: 2003. Use the Zodiac Checker to confirm yours.

What time of day belongs to the Goat?

The Goat rules the double hour 13:00–15:00, the wèi hour. A traditional explanation is that this is a good time for goats to graze, after the heat of noon has passed.

Why is the Goat 8th in the zodiac?

The order follows the twelve Earthly Branches, where the Goat is wèi. The popular explanation is the Great Race folk tale: the Goat did not cross alone but shared a raft with the Monkey and the Rooster, and the three helped each other reach the far bank, arriving eighth, ninth and tenth.

What is the Goat's fixed element?

Earth (Yin). It is separate from your birth-year element, which is also Earth only in 1979 and 2039.

វិញ្ញាណនៃមមែ

តាមប្រពៃណី មមែតំណាងឱ្យភាពទន់ភ្លន់ ការច្នៃប្រឌិត និងបេះដូងដែលយកចិត្តទុកដាក់។

ស្វែងយល់ឱ្យកាន់តែជ្រៅអំពីមមែ

មមែ (ជួនកាលហៅថា ចៀម) ជាសិល្បករទន់ភ្លន់នៃនិមិត្តសញ្ញា ទាក់ទាញទៅរកសោភណភាព សុខដុមរមនា និងអារម្មណ៍ជ្រៅ។ មមែសម្គាល់អារម្មណ៍ដែលអ្នកដទៃមើលមិនឃើញ ធ្វើឱ្យពួកគេជាមិត្តភក្តិកក់ក្តៅ និងពូកែក្នុងវិស័យច្នៃប្រឌិត។ ពួកគេធ្វើការបានល្អបំផុតក្នុងបរិយាកាសដែលមានសុវត្ថិភាព និងការគាំទ្រ ហើយងាយនឹងពិបាកនៅក្នុងបរិយាកាសប្រកួតប្រជែងខ្លាំង។ ដោយគ្មានការគាំទ្រនោះ មមែអាចក្លាយជាស្ទាក់ស្ទើរ ឬពឹងផ្អែកលើអ្នកដទៃឱ្យធ្វើការសម្រេចចិត្តពិបាកៗជំនួសគេ។

បុគ្គលិកលក្ខណៈ

សុភាពរាបសា ច្នៃប្រឌិត និងអាណិតអាសូរ។ មមែមានអារម្មណ៍សិល្បៈខ្លាំង និងយកចិត្តទុកដាក់ចំពោះអ្នកដទៃ។

មមែសម្គាល់ភាពស្រស់ស្អាត និងអារម្មណ៍ជ្រៅដែលមនុស្សភាគច្រើនដើរកាត់ដោយមិនបានឃើញ ដែលធ្វើឱ្យពួកគេជាដៃគូកក់ក្តៅ និងពូកែពិតប្រាកដក្នុងការងារច្នៃប្រឌិត ឬថែទាំ។ ពួកគេលូតលាស់លឿនបំផុតនៅជុំវិញមនុស្សដែលអត់ធ្មត់ជាមួយភាពរសើបនោះ ជាជាងអន់ចិត្តចង់ឱ្យពួកគេរឹងមាំជាងនេះ។

它在过河比赛中的名次
