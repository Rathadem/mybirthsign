2011 Chinese Zodiac: Year of the Metal Rabbit

Born in 2011? Your Chinese zodiac animal is most likely the Rabbit, but check the date: the 2011 Metal Rabbit year began on 3 February 2011. Here is everything about the year, from its element to its lucky colors and best matches.

2011: a Yin Metal Rabbit year

The Chinese zodiac year 2011 was the Year of the Rabbit. It began with Lunar New Year on 3 February 2011 and ended on 22 January 2012, the eve of the next Lunar New Year.

Each year also has an element and a Yin or Yang quality. 2011 ends in 1, which makes it a Metal year, and as an odd year it is Yin. In the traditional stem-and-branch count, 2011 is a Xin-mao year. The same Metal Rabbit combination comes round only once every 60 years: 1951 before it and 2071 after it.

Born in January or February 2011?

Lunar New Year 2011 fell on 3 February 2011, so everyone born in January 2011, and in February before that date, belongs to the previous year's animal, the Tiger.

The zodiac year starts at Lunar New Year, not on 1 January, which is why early-year birthdays need checking. Our guide Born in January? explains the rule, and the zodiac sign checker gives the exact answer for any birth date.

The Rabbit's personality

Our Rabbit guide describes the sign like this: “Gentle, thoughtful, and diplomatic. Rabbits value harmony and are skilled at avoiding conflict.” The usual watch-out: “Can be overly cautious and avoid necessary confrontation.”

The Metal element adds its own flavour. Metal years bring discipline, ambition, and strong will. People of this element tend to be determined, independent, and value-driven. Read more in our Metal element guide, or the full Year of the Rabbit page.

Careers often linked with the Rabbit: diplomacy, counseling, design, hospitality.

Lucky colors, numbers and days for the Rabbit

  • Lucky colors: pink, red and purple
  • Lucky numbers: 3, 4 and 6
  • Lucky days: Monday and Thursday

These are the values used across this site. For a lucky color, number and time that change every day, see the daily fortune.

Best matches for someone born in 2011

In traditional compatibility, the Rabbit's best matches are the Goat, Pig and Dog, and its clash sign is the Rooster, which sits directly opposite it on the zodiac circle. Our compatibility calculator compares two full birth dates, elements included.

The Rabbit in 2026 and 2027

2026 (Fire Horse, until 5 February 2027): for the Rabbit, it has no special traditional relationship with the Horse, so 2026 is a neutral year.

2027 (Fire Goat, from 6 February 2027): for the Rabbit, it shares the Goat's triangle (Rabbit, Goat and Pig), so 2027 is traditionally favourable. See Chinese zodiac 2027 predictions for every sign.

Other Year of the Rabbit years

Years of the same animal come every twelve years, each with a different element: 1987 (Fire Rabbit); 1999 (Earth Rabbit); 2023 (Water Rabbit); 2035 (Wood Rabbit). The full list is in our Chinese zodiac years chart.
