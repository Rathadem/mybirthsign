# 12 Zodiac Characters — master prompts (Disney/Pixar-inspired golden style)

Make each character once (square 1:1), keep the pictures, and add them as reference images ("ingredients") in Flow for every video, so each animal always looks the same.
If a tool refuses brand names, remove "inspired by … Disney and Pixar-style" — the rest of the description keeps the look.

## Rat (ជូត)
Create a premium-quality 3D animated fantasy character of an adorable golden rat with big sparkling eyes, tiny jade-green ear jewels and a proud little smile, the Rat of the Chinese zodiac, inspired by the charm, expressive storytelling and enchanting visual appeal of classic Disney and Pixar-style animated family films. Give the character oversized sparkling eyes, an adorable expressive face, soft rounded features, a small cute nose, beautifully shaped proportions and a warm, lovable personality. Combine whimsical character design with luxurious golden craftsmanship: polished gold textures, intricate engraved ornaments, luminous highlights, delicate pink and gold accents and beautifully sculpted details. The character should feel alive, emotionally expressive, magical, cute and majestic, with a memorable silhouette and a high-end theatrical animation finish. Place the full-body character on an ornate pink-and-gold lotus pedestal, surrounded by a glowing full moon, a deep indigo starry sky, a softly illuminated Khmer temple inspired by Angkor Wat, floating lotus flowers, magical sparkles and warm golden reflections. Use cinematic lighting, rich colors, soft atmospheric glow, realistic metallic reflections, smooth 3D materials, beautiful depth of field and professional animated-movie-quality rendering. Composition: square 1:1, full body visible, three-quarter view, centered character, balanced framing, consistent design across all 12 Chinese zodiac animals, with space around the character. Restrictions: no text, no letters, no watermark, no logo, no cropped body parts.
